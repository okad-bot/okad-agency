How much do UGC videos cost in 2026?

Short answer: a single UGC video from a creator usually costs $150 to $300 before usage rights and editing. Marketplace data puts the average single video at around $150 to $212 (Influee, PPC.io). A finished, tested ad with script, casting and editing costs more, and gets cheaper when you order in volume.

Below is what changes the price, what you actually pay for, and what my own prices look like.

What drives the price

Usage rights

Organic posting is cheaper than paid ads. Paid usage for 3, 6 or 12 months is often 20 to 50% on top.

Whitelisting

Running ads from the creator's account costs extra but often lowers CPA. See whitelisting.

Hook variations

3 to 5 openings per video multiply your tests for a small extra cost.

Raw vs edited

Raw clips are cheap, but someone still has to edit them into ads.

Niche

Health, beauty, finance and alcohol need compliance-aware scripts and cost more.

Volume

10 videos cost less per video than 1. 50 a month cost less again (how brands get 50+ videos a month).

Why the cheapest video is rarely the cheapest ad

The cost nobody counts: losing videos

Most UGC videos don't win. If 1 in 5 becomes a winner, your real price is the cost of five videos, not one. That's why cheap videos without testing often end up expensive. The fix is a testing system, not cheaper creators. My Meta creative testing framework.

FAQ

How much should a UGC video cost?

For a single creator video, $150 to $300 is the common range. A finished, tested ad from an agency is about $100 to $500 depending on volume and what's included.

Are usage rights included?

Often not. Always check if the price covers paid ads and for how long.

Is AI UGC cheaper?

Yes, usually several times cheaper per video, but it works best for testing angles, not for testimonials.

How many UGC videos do I need?

For most brands spending on Meta, 10 to 20 new ads every one to two weeks. See how many variations.
