When OKAD is better

Choose OKAD when you need strategy, production, and testing handled by one team. You don't have to write briefs, manage creators, or figure out what to test — that's the whole point.

  • You want someone to own the creative strategy, not just shoot clips.
  • You need professional editing — cuts, captions, pacing, music.
  • You're running paid ads and need a testing framework to find winners.
  • You want a team that learns what works for your brand over time.

When Billo is better

Billo works well when you already know exactly what you want, can write your own briefs, and just need someone to record the footage.

  • You need a quick, one-off UGC clip and have the brief ready.
  • Your budget is under $500 and you only need 1–3 videos.
  • You have an in-house editor and just need raw creator footage.
  • You're comfortable managing the creative process yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Is OKAD more expensive than Billo?

Not per video. OKAD's batch pricing starts at $500 for 10 videos ($50/video), which is cheaper than Billo's $59–$159 per video. The difference is that OKAD includes strategy, scripting, and professional editing — Billo gives you the raw clip.

Do I need to manage creators with OKAD?

No. OKAD handles creator selection, briefing, communication, and quality control. With Billo, you browse the marketplace, select creators, write the brief, and manage revisions yourself.

Can I use Billo for paid ads?

You can, but Billo doesn't include ad strategy or performance testing. You'll get raw UGC clips that you'll need to edit, caption, and test on your own. OKAD delivers ad-ready videos with a testing framework built in.

Which is better for scaling ad creative?

OKAD. Scaling requires systematic testing — different hooks, angles, creators, and formats. OKAD's model is designed for this: produce a batch, test on real spend, identify winners, and iterate. Billo is a one-video-at-a-time marketplace without a testing layer.
