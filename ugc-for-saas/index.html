Why SaaS video ads are different

Your product is hard to show

A dashboard or workflow tool doesn't have a physical unboxing moment. You need screen recordings, walkthroughs and motion that make the product click in 3 seconds.

Long sales cycles need more touches

SaaS buyers don't impulse-buy. Ads need to work across the funnel: awareness, consideration, trial. One video won't cover all three.

Polished demos feel like ads

A studio-produced product tour gets skipped. Native-looking content from a real user gets watched. But "real" still needs to be scripted around the right pain point.

Small audiences fatigue fast

B2B audiences on Meta and LinkedIn are narrow. One winning ad loses steam in a week or two. You need a pipeline of fresh creative, not a single hero video.

Formats that work for SaaS

Screen recording + voiceover

Your product in action on a real device, with a voiceover that leads with the benefit, not the feature. The fastest format to produce and often the highest performer for tools with strong UI.

Creator walkthrough

A real person on camera, using the product and reacting. Bridges the trust gap: viewers see someone they relate to getting value. Works especially well for TikTok and Reels.

Problem-solution

Open with a pain the viewer recognizes ("I used to track everything in spreadsheets"), show the product solving it. Scripts come from customer reviews and support tickets.

Before and after workflow

Split-screen or sequential: the old way versus the new way. Makes the time savings or complexity reduction visible and visceral.

Canvas and B-roll

Text on screen, product screenshots in motion, no creator face needed. Cheap to test, fast to iterate, works well for retargeting. See my Canvas & Tech UGC guide.

Founder or team member

The founder explains why they built the product. Authentic, high-trust, especially effective for early-stage SaaS going after a niche audience.

FAQ

Does UGC work for B2B SaaS?

Yes. Decision-makers scroll the same feeds as everyone else. A creator saying "this tool cut my reporting time in half" works the same way a consumer testimonial does — it is social proof in native format. The targeting is harder, but the creative principles are the same.

What about LinkedIn video ads?

I produce content that works on LinkedIn too: 4:5 or 1:1, sound-off friendly, with clear text overlays. LinkedIn audiences watch less but click more deliberately, so the hook and CTA matter even more.

Can you use our existing product screenshots?

Yes. Canvas and B-roll formats are built from screenshots, screen recordings and existing assets. No new shoot needed. This is the cheapest way to start testing.

How do you handle complex products?

I focus on one feature and one pain point per video. A full product tour belongs on your website, not in an ad. Each ad answers one question: "What does this do for me?"

What if we already have a demo video?

A polished demo is not an ad. But I can use the same screen recordings as raw material, re-edit them with hooks, captions and native pacing, and turn one demo into 5 to 10 testable ads.
