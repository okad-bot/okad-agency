When OKAD is better

OKAD is the right choice when you want a team to own the entire creative pipeline — from strategy through tested, ad-ready videos. No brief-writing, no creator management, no post-production on your end.

  • You don't have time to manage creators and write briefs yourself.
  • You want scripts, hooks, and angles crafted by a creative team.
  • You need professional editing, not raw clips you still have to cut.
  • You want a testing framework that tells you which ads actually work.

When Insense is better

Insense is a good fit if you have an in-house creative team that can write briefs, manage creator relationships, and handle editing internally. The marketplace gives you scale and direct access.

  • You have a creative strategist who writes briefs and scripts in-house.
  • You want direct relationships with creators for ongoing collaborations.
  • You need access to a large pool of creators across many niches.
  • You have editors on your team to handle post-production.

Frequently asked questions

Is OKAD or Insense cheaper?

It depends on volume. Insense charges a platform fee ($400+/month) plus individual creator fees. OKAD charges per batch starting at $500 for 10 videos with strategy, editing, and testing included. For brands producing regularly, OKAD's all-inclusive pricing is often more cost-effective than Insense's layered fees.

Does Insense include video editing?

No. Insense connects you with creators who deliver raw clips. You handle editing, captions, and post-production yourself or with your own team. OKAD delivers fully edited, ad-ready videos with captions, pacing, and music.

Can I use Insense creators for whitelisted ads?

Yes, Insense supports creator whitelisting for Meta ads, which is one of its strengths. OKAD also supports whitelisted ads through its creator network, with the added benefit of handling the campaign strategy and creative testing.

Which is better for a small team with no creative strategist?

OKAD. If you don't have someone in-house to write briefs, select creators, and plan creative angles, Insense will require significant time investment. OKAD handles all of that — you review and approve, the team does the rest.
