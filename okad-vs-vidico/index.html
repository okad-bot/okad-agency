When to choose OKAD

OKAD is the better fit if you're a DTC or e-commerce brand that needs to test a high volume of creative concepts quickly. The model is built around iteration: shoot 10 ad variations, test them on real spend, kill the losers, scale the winners, and repeat.

  • You need 10–50 ad variations per month, not one hero video.
  • You want strategy, scripting, production, and testing from one team.
  • You're optimizing for ROAS and need data-driven creative decisions.
  • You want a fast turnaround so you can iterate weekly.

When to choose Vidico

Vidico is the better choice when you need a polished brand film, a product launch video, or high-end explainer content. Their strength is cinematic production quality and storytelling.

  • You need a single high-production brand video or product film.
  • Your priority is production value over iteration speed.
  • You have a larger budget and a longer timeline.
  • You want a studio-grade look with professional actors and sets.

Frequently asked questions

Is OKAD cheaper than Vidico?

Yes, significantly. OKAD's batch model starts at $500 for 10 videos, while Vidico's custom projects typically start at $5,000 and up. The difference reflects the production style: OKAD optimizes for volume testing, Vidico for premium single-asset production.

Can Vidico do UGC-style ads?

Vidico can incorporate UGC elements, but it's not their core model. They work through partner networks rather than maintaining a dedicated creator roster. OKAD has 600+ vetted UGC creators ready to shoot within days.

Which agency is better for Meta and TikTok ads?

OKAD is purpose-built for paid social ads on Meta, TikTok, and YouTube. The entire workflow — scripts, creators, editing, and testing — is designed around what performs on these platforms. Vidico produces content that can run on any platform but doesn't specialize in performance optimization.

Do either agency offer creative strategy?

OKAD includes creative strategy and a testing framework as part of every engagement. You get data on what hooks, angles, and formats work best for your brand. Vidico focuses on production execution — strategy and media buying are typically handled by the brand or a separate agency.
