What to look for in a UGC ad agency

Ask who writes the scripts

If the answer is "the creator", you are buying footage, not ads.

Ask what happens after launch

A good agency reads hook rate, CTR and CPA and changes the next batch.

Ask for hook variations

3 to 5 per video should be standard.

Check usage rights

Paid ad usage and its length should be in the price.

Start small

A first batch of 10 shows more than any sales call.

Look at cases with numbers

Not just a reel of nice videos.
