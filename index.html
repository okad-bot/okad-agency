Questions people ask on calls

How fast do I get the first ads?

Re-edits, AI videos and statics in 5 business days. UGC with creators takes 2 to 3 weeks, because we send them your product first.

Do you have a team?

Yes, a small one. I do the strategy, every script and a big part of the editing myself. Two people help me: one runs UGC casting from our 3,000+ creators, the other does graphics and AI generation.

Are creator fees included?

Yes, the price per video includes the creator.

Who owns the ads?

You do. Run them on any paid channel. If you want Spark or partnership ads from a creator's account, I arrange it with the creator.

Do you run the ads?

No, I make the ads and the test plan. You or your media buyer launch them. If you don't have one, I'll show you how to set up the test and can recommend a media buyer.

How do I pay?

The test is paid upfront. Monthly plans are paid month by month, with no long contract.

Which languages?

Any. I have made ads in English, German and many other languages, with native creators.

Is it AI or real people?

Both. Before we start I tell you which videos are AI and which are filmed by creators.

What if I don’t like a script?

You approve every script before anyone films or edits, and one round of edits is included in every batch.

What if nothing works?

Then we know which angles don't sell. The next batch tests new ones, and I re-cut the best ads with new hooks first, because that is the cheapest next test.
