Questions

Can we work without a call?

Yes. Send me a link to your product and any footage. I reply by email with what I'd test first.

How fast do I get the first ads?

First ads in 5 business days. UGC with new creators takes 2 to 3 weeks.

What if nothing works?

Some ads will lose, that's what a test is for. At the start I ask how bold you want to go: safer ideas built on what already works for you and your competitors, or new ideas that can win big or flop. At minimum you get a tested answer. At best, a new winner. Tests need enough volume, so I plan them with enough ads.

Where are your creators?

In every big market: the US, UK, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and more. Any age, look and background. Any language.

Are creator fees included?

No, they come on top and depend on the brief. I tell you the full price before we start.

Do you run the ads?

No, your team or media buyer runs them. I read the results and plan the next batch.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Yes, any time with one month's notice.

How do I pay?

100% upfront for each test or month.

What is the smallest order?

The $1,000 test. For ads without strategy, Production only starts at $2,500 a month.
