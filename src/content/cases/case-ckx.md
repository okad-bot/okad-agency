ChainKeyX is a Singapore-based DeFi platform offering crypto exchange, storage, and lending services. With investment rounds ahead — including one with J.P. Morgan — they needed a digital presence that could explain the platform, build trust, and scale with the product.

## The Brief

ChainKeyX needed more than a website. They needed:

- A landing page that could replace traditional investor decks
- A UX/UI foundation scalable with new features
- A trustworthy brand identity making crypto feel clear, safe, and intelligent
- A Framer-built site with speed, interactivity, and polish

## Why Web3 Startups Need Strong UX

DeFi users are smart but wary. With thousands of projects launching daily, the bar is high:

- **New users need clarity** — interfaces must onboard without confusion
- **Investors judge within seconds** — if the product doesn't feel legit, the round is lost
- **Scalable design systems are non-negotiable** — the product will evolve and the design must support that

## Website Design: Bento Style + Framer Development

![ChainKeyX bento-style website layout with modular sections](https://framerusercontent.com/images/sJt6gXd7dWxPVfTR1PlylyxdxU.jpg?width=2800&height=1860)

We built a responsive, modular landing page in bento-box layout, prioritizing:

- **Visual simplicity** with Web3-native UI language
- **Lightweight animations** to communicate tech confidence
- **Responsive layout** that grows with the product
- **Clear feature presentation** covering use cases and security

## UX/UI System for the Platform

To prepare for long-term product growth, we developed:

- A design system standardizing buttons, forms, data tables, and flows
- 10+ UX interaction scenarios built in Figma for key user journeys
- User journey improvements based on competitor analysis
- Data-heavy UI balancing readability and depth

## Challenges We Solved

![ChainKeyX platform UX/UI design system and user flows](https://framerusercontent.com/images/UhKHl8oBRRD9tL2KLLdJpWUkI14.jpg?width=2800&height=6261)

- **Cross-cultural design sensitivity (Asia + Global)** — Asian crypto markets value clean, structured aesthetics. We adapted visual cues and microcopy accordingly.
- **Global team communication** — Different accents, time zones, and async updates made miscommunication a risk. We documented everything, recorded calls, and kept sprint cycles tight.
- **Investor-first thinking** — Every section answered: "Why should I care?" and "Can I trust this?"

## Results

- ChainKeyX launched a fully responsive, modular Framer site
- A scalable platform UI ready for future product rollouts
- The website played a key role in securing investment from J.P. Morgan
- The design system allows internal product teams to iterate independently
