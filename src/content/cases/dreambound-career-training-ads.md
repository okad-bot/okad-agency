**Ten ads. Strict rules.** Dreambound helps people find training for jobs in health care: medical billing, phlebotomy, medical assistant. The brief was strict: grab attention fast, sell one program, never promise a job, never name a school.

## What I delivered

- **10 ads** across 3 platforms (TikTok, Meta, Snapchat)
- Hooks and scripts, editing, AI voiceover
- **1 program per ad** — clear focus
- Talking heads, podcast-style, and POV formats
- Screen recordings of the site search as the CTA
