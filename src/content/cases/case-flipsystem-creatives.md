Flip System is a CRM platform built for real estate professionals who flip, rent, or sell properties. They needed a large-scale series of performance-driven creatives for paid campaigns — content that could engage, educate, and convert while maintaining a strong, flexible brand identity across platforms.

## Project Overview

![Flip System CRM platform ad creative project overview](https://framerusercontent.com/images/DZQ6Ze3nSuQDrY5WJSB02U.png?width=2800&height=1861)

We were tasked with developing both static and motion content for paid campaigns. This meant clarity, speed, visual consistency, and a high volume of iteration.

**Creative goals:**

- Develop a high-volume package of graphics for paid campaigns
- Produce motion creatives that communicate product value quickly
- Maintain brand consistency while introducing stylistic variety
- Optimize for performance across formats and platforms

![Flip System ad creative brand guidelines and design system](https://framerusercontent.com/images/EjfP5XZar8Z5dCsZ9cG65ESHcpY.png?width=2800&height=1861)

## Visual Language, Then Volume

![Flip System design process and visual framework development](https://framerusercontent.com/images/cWybDUfD5qGCu1U5SdgTKXVL3I.png?width=2800&height=1861)

Before scaling production, we locked in the visual framework: color palette, typography, illustration guidelines, and logo handling. From there, we built over 100 ad variations — experimenting with layout, typography scale, and illustration styles — all using the same copy for efficient A/B testing.

**Key tactics:**

- **Design remixing:** one core idea rendered in multiple visual styles
- **Multiple design systems** based on a single value prop
- **Niche visual triggers:** floor plans, keys, dashboards, and other real estate imagery

![Flip System static ad creative variations for A/B testing](https://framerusercontent.com/images/kTuT9vpYXdTi1AUSWVKsx8hWNY.png?width=2800&height=1401)

## Motion Content That Converts

Motion design is often overlooked or overproduced. We struck a balance: dynamic explainer-style animations that simplified the CRM's core benefits. These were modular and platform-ready.

**Key elements:**

- **User experience mirroring:** scenes built around real product workflows
- **Scroll-friendly transitions:** soft motion for feed-based platforms
- **Animated UI walkthroughs:** mimicking in-product interaction
- **Format flexibility:** sound-on/off versions, voiceover vs music-only, vertical vs horizontal

![Flip System motion ad creative and animated explainer examples](https://framerusercontent.com/images/wOOVJyTVDVxIwzaGSsAwnNmWXc.png?width=2800&height=1861)

## Performance Optimization Through Design

Because the Flip System team was already running campaigns, we focused on ad variants designed for performance tracking: thumbnail changes, CTA positioning, and micro-animations as click triggers. The goal was not just to look good, but to outperform previous rounds of content.

![Flip System ad performance optimization and CTA testing variants](https://framerusercontent.com/images/JJqBaTTylhxd43xpWfeSEosgI.png?width=2800&height=1861)

## Results

- **100+** performance-focused static creatives delivered
- **Multiple motion creatives** used across paid campaigns
- **Increased engagement** on educational video ads
- **Repeatable creative system** established for future iterations
- **Ongoing partnership** — Flip System continued working with us for new campaigns
