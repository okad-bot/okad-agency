## Rebranding from Scratch: How to Launch a Franchise That Sells Itself

Discover how Jumpman’s franchise-ready website design doubled class bookings, boosted session time, and streamlined partner onboarding with immersive UX, theatrical transitions, and mobile-first booking. Learn our strategic web architecture and modular components to scale fitness studios effortlessly. Ready to transform your site? Explore now!

If your website doesn’t sell for you, it’s silently killing your business. A modern digital brand can’t afford to have a site that simply exists — it has to perform. It has to convert first-timers into regulars, answer questions before they’re asked, and reflect your brand energy from the very first scroll.

Jumpman, the first trampoline fitness studio in Paris, had been growing steadily since 2016. With new locations opening and interest from potential partners, the brand needed a clear foundation for franchise expansion. Their priority? A bold rebrand and a website that spoke fluently to two key groups:

B2C visitors – everyday clients looking to book a session.

B2B partners – potential franchisees evaluating the business.

The website had to feel immersive, move fast, and make you want to jump—literally.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/O8CXC4a9wGlJtiGTKBaNGrDgGoA.jpg?width=1400&height=1859)


## Strategic Website Goals

We worked with Jumpman to define the four non-negotiables for the new website:

Frictionless Booking in 2 Clicks – Booking a class had to feel as easy as jumping.

High-Impact Atmosphere – From the homepage, visitors needed to feel the drama, the lights, and the movement.

Guidance for Newcomers – A dedicated “First Time?” page, answering the unspoken fears of newcomers.

Franchise-Ready Infrastructure – Clear brand positioning, strategic copywriting, and visual storytelling that speaks to investors.


## Key UX & Web Design Features

We crafted a full end-to-end web experience, combining UX strategy, visual storytelling, and conversion optimization:

Navigation optimized for B2C first-timers and B2B researchers.

Custom UI design with layered visuals and theatrical transitions to support the brand’s "Phantom Stage" identity.

Responsive animations to reflect movement and energy across devices.

Integrated videos that mirror live sessions—lights, jumps, shadows, and sweat.

Modular components to make future franchise launches faster and cheaper.

Every design choice reinforced the theatrical identity of the brand while staying focused on UX performance.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/HcUbDoNJQXj59IEy6bxqLytjR28.jpg?width=1400&height=4682)


## Design That Converts: Real UX, Not Just Pretty Pixels

We designed for performance, not just aesthetics. That meant:

Clear CTAs always within reach

Minimalistic forms with auto-validation

Mobile-first booking experience

Fast-loading visual elements despite complex animations


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/h6MFw86zKc8IEzQevNkQvYrd8.jpg?width=1400&height=1948)


## Results: A Website That Works as Hard as the Brand

Doubled B2C bookings within the first month

Franchise materials integrated seamlessly into the site — boosting credibility and streamlining onboarding

Session time increased by 54%, with users spending more time exploring videos and testimonials

Reduced drop-off rate by 41% thanks to a smoother funnel and visual storytelling


### Final Thoughts

Your website isn’t just your digital business card. It’s your best sales rep, your franchise pitch, and your community magnet. If your digital touchpoint doesn’t do the heavy lifting—no one else will.

If you’re preparing to scale, launch in a new market, or shift your positioning, a strategic website isn’t optional—it’s the foundation. That’s what made Jumpman’s site a lever for growth, not a bottleneck.
