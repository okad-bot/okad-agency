SLP is a community for entrepreneurs that blends business club, accelerator, and educational programs around peer-to-peer learning. We were brought in to develop the brand identity and a full content strategy to grow their online presence and member engagement.

![SLP community event with entrepreneurs networking and collaborating](https://framerusercontent.com/images/y8rFHbiCEwwggpGOq9er9IFkCc8.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)

The community stands apart through its foundation in peer-to-peer learning: alumni from previous cohorts lead sessions, and participants shape the agenda by inviting speakers and requesting specific topics.

![SLP program branding and visual identity materials](https://framerusercontent.com/images/q88GlNpyVP9G3n9wPVp3E188Lk.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)

![SLP branded content examples for social media channels](https://framerusercontent.com/images/YU5C9pYp0O132LWJA4SXD7Yuck.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)


## The Concept Behind SLP

SLP is not just a business club or accelerator — it's a unique community where participants share entrepreneurial and personal experiences. The program includes mandatory learning modules, but participants can also create their own educational path based on their interests and business needs. This approach is centered around openness, mutual support, and collaborative problem-solving, ensuring the community continues to thrive even after the formal program ends.

![SLP peer-to-peer learning session with entrepreneurs presenting](https://framerusercontent.com/images/3WGbFEIAtHeJVQseaZdm0sFR7qw.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)

![SLP community members during a workshop discussion](https://framerusercontent.com/images/DOrG0kbH34avPcwQMblxqOkck.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)


## Challenges and Strategy

When we took on branding and content for SLP, we needed to go beyond traditional marketing. The challenge: build a community brand that resonates with entrepreneurs while staying true to SLP's core values of openness and collaboration.

![SLP brand strategy analysis and positioning document](https://framerusercontent.com/images/ae4oQsBzYjDP5Mb1P5YwAsApo00.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)

![SLP competitive analysis and audience personas overview](https://framerusercontent.com/images/83g0RoUGxpzE9udksC1Lio5AMM.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)


## Deep Analysis and Strategy Development

We started with a thorough analysis of the SLP program, its internal processes, and participant interactions. Key steps included:

- **Competitor research** — identified gaps and opportunities in the entrepreneur community space
- **Persona development** — mapped the brand's core values and audience needs
- **Content format evaluation** — assessed effectiveness and production cost of each format to ensure long-term sustainability

![SLP content strategy framework and brand value mapping](https://framerusercontent.com/images/V6TluFx64VmCPSjjsyyLjAvc0.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)


## Crafting the Content Strategy

With the analysis complete, we created a comprehensive content strategy divided into two main categories:

- **Video posts** — capturing real community moments and success stories
- **Graphic posts** — educational and informational content with consistent branding

Each content type had its own guidelines and posting frequency. We also designed a visual identity overhaul for SLP's social media channels and created platform-specific content plans for Instagram and Telegram.

![SLP content plan and publishing calendar for social media](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Qg9Q0EuXJuz8bpDDzNtsY1pZk.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)


## Production: Making the Content Come Alive

Creating content for a peer-to-peer community meant producing authentic, high-quality material across multiple formats:

- **Video production** — organized a large-scale shoot during a graduation event, resulting in 50+ professionally edited videos with subtitles
- **Event documentation** — filmed a two-day event in Yerevan with a professional operator
- **Regular content creation** — consistently produced a mix of informational posts about the program and educational materials for founders and entrepreneurs

![SLP video production setup during community graduation event](https://framerusercontent.com/images/K9P9O4oS3sm8geX7tysoV3oiM.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)


## Text and Communication

The agency founder took an active role in writing texts, ensuring they resonated with the community's values. The messaging was emotionally compelling, relatable, and tailored to provide practical insights for entrepreneurs.

![SLP social media posts and copywriting examples](https://framerusercontent.com/images/fnXdBWkQIbrZYWyN8ynqKXz1pkc.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)

We also handled ongoing communication management, keeping SLP's social media channels dynamic and fostering continuous audience interaction.


## Additional Activities

Beyond content creation, the agency organized events for the SLP community and created passive integration and advertising content — further expanding the reach of SLP's messaging.

![SLP community event organized by the agency with participants networking](https://framerusercontent.com/images/HAy1Lk8ljLrPAnXc8haIpz9ckRI.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)


## Results

The SLP community's online presence grew significantly:

- **Increased engagement** — higher interaction rates across social media platforms
- **Stronger brand perception** — improved visual and informational quality of social media profiles
- **Connected community** — members felt more supported and engaged through consistent content
- **Long-term growth** — steady flow of relevant content enhanced member participation and program visibility
