**48.9K YouTube views in three weeks.** Fragstore sells action figures, statues and limited-edition collectibles. Their audience lives on YouTube and TikTok, watching unboxings, shelf tours and first-look reviews. The brand needed a content engine that could fill both platforms without running a shoot every week.

We booked one shoot day at their showroom, filmed a creator walking through the shelves, unboxing new arrivals and reacting to rare pieces — then cut the footage into 40 videos optimized for each platform.

## What we delivered

- **40 videos from one shoot day** — unboxings, shelf tours, product spotlights
- **48.9K YouTube views** in the first three weeks
- **~2x TikTok profile views** after the first batch went live
- Formats built to reuse: same footage, different hooks, different lengths

One day of shooting, weeks of content.
