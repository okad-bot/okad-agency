## The Challenge: Marketing an Automotive Brand to a Niche Community

Cyber Pulse is an automotive content and community brand built for the Russian-speaking audience in Portugal. The challenge? Build a content machine that generates trust, traffic, and ultimately — leads.

Unlike mass-market car dealerships or auto blogs, Cyber Pulse needed to:

Educate a niche audience about complex car import logistics

Build trust in a community-first format

Convert content views into car requests and purchases

Do all this in two languages — Russian and English — for different segments


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Z0v42LV3coMFFOcHPYPh2eUqFY.jpg?width=2800&height=2072)


### Our Strategy: Build the Brand Like a Media Company

We didn’t just run ads. We built an ecosystem. Here's how:


### 1. Content Planning & Editorial Strategy

We developed a detailed content plan based on:

Local insights from our Lisbon-based team

Seasonal trends in auto imports

Audience pain points (taxes, insurance, legal processes)

We sourced a copywriter with deep automotive knowledge, designed all visuals in line with the brand book, and tailored every post to platform behavior — what works in Telegram doesn’t always work on Instagram.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/X1WiOJLi9QFXIV4SdNJ3t3Vhyc.jpg?width=2800&height=1860)


### 2. Full Content Team and Workflow

We assembled a hybrid team of:

Content Manager (Lisbon-based) — strategic oversight and planning

Automotive Copywriter — expert writing and research

Designer — car visuals, infographics, Instagram assets

Telegram + Notion for collaboration — to keep it all streamlined

Regular strategy calls with the client to review performance and tweak direction


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Q8IgqRaW2gkSXbcQLXyqXeBADI.png?width=2800&height=1860)


### 3. Multiformat, Platform-Native Content

We published content daily across:

Telegram Channel – articles, tips, updates, client stories

Telegram Chat – a live, engaged community of enthusiasts and leads

Instagram – storytelling, car visuals, highlights in English and Russian

Infographics & Case Studies – simplifying complex topics visually


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/NRAP4jDeHRA5Cz1yQ7pYR3p9FY.jpg?width=2800&height=1916)


### 4. Paid Distribution with Real-Time Optimization

We managed advertising placement in 10–30 Telegram channels per month. That meant:

Negotiating rates

Tracking cost-per-click, cost-per-lead, and cost-per-sale

Weekly reporting and campaign optimization

We also ran Meta Ads targeting expats and Russian-speaking users in Portugal.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/THOVnKVlKCEIdzANEEMawdMpnGw.jpg?width=2800&height=1646)


### 5. Funnel-Oriented Content Ecosystem

We designed a content funnel that moved users from attention to conversion:

Top of funnel: Engaging car stories, community buzz, useful tips

Middle of funnel: Real client cases, testimonials, before/after

Bottom of funnel: Telegram chat access, price breakdowns, car import timelines

Automation: Lead qualification through Telegram bots and email flows


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Kg8kb172KFuILMh6R1UgS8zF8.jpg?width=2800&height=1602)


### Results After 12 Months

From 0 to 1500+ subscribers in both Telegram channel and chat

36,000+ Instagram followers with consistently high engagement

Significant lead growth directly from content

Optimized ad spend through tracking and iterative testing

Automated lead capture through bots and flows

All without a single cold call.

### What You Can Learn from This

Treat your brand like a media outlet. Daily content builds trust — and trust drives conversions.

Use Telegram as more than a broadcast tool. It can be a fully functioning sales funnel.

Instagram is not dead. With the right visuals and strategy, it's still powerful for multilingual, multicultural audiences.

Case content is your sales engine. Real stories, real outcomes, real questions answered.

You don’t need a huge budget — you need consistency, structure, and story.
