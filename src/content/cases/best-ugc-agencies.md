## How to choose a UGC agency

1. **Ask who writes the scripts.** If the answer is "the creator", you are buying footage, not ads.
2. **Ask what happens after launch.** A good agency reads hook rate, CTR and CPA and changes the next batch.
3. **Ask for hook variations.** 3 to 5 per video should be standard.
4. **Check usage rights.** Paid ad usage and its length should be in the price.
5. **Start small.** A first batch of 10 shows more than any sales call.
6. **Look at cases with numbers,** not just a reel of nice videos.
