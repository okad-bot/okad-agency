**A US consumer AI app needed video ads that sell.** In four months of creative testing on Meta we went from two videos that had ever worked to 160+ ads — and let real sales pick the winners.

| Period | Scope | Not our scope |
|---|---|---|
| April–August 2026 · pilot + 2 testing batches | Research, strategy, scripts, casting, production, analysis | Media buying — the client ran the account |

## Why weren't their Meta ads working?

The app turns a few selfies into professional headshots and wanted to roughly double its Meta budget. Almost all ads were still images, all of them fatigued; only one or two videos had ever worked, top ads were seen twice by the same people, and nobody could say *why* a winner won.

**The brief:** find video winners across audiences, learn which message, format and face sell, keep a pipeline of fresh hypotheses, hand over data the team can act on.

## How do you know what to say in an ad?

We don't guess — we read what customers already wrote: buyer surveys, reviews, comments under the ads, past ad results and competitors. About half of the first 70 concepts trace back to a specific data point.

- **Where the "before" photo came from** (a car, a couch, a bathroom mirror) → creators used their real old selfies next to the result.
- **The #1 fear in comments: "will they recognise me?"** → the message became "same person, same face, just professionally lit".
- **Most buyers are working professionals using the photo on LinkedIn** → scripts name real jobs and show the headshot where it lives.

## Why test 70 video ads instead of 5?

Nobody can predict the winner. Batch 1: **8 angles × 6 formats × 2 creators, 15 hypotheses, 70 concepts**, 3 awareness levels, gender-swapped scripts to isolate the creator effect, `Angle_Format_Creator_Hook` naming for instant analysis.

| Angle | Example hook |
|---|---|
| Pain | "I've been putting this off for two years." |
| Before / after | "How I turned my worst photo into my best LinkedIn headshot." |
| Speed | "15 minutes. From opening the site to a new LinkedIn photo." |
| Occasion | "I had 48 hours before the interview…" |
| Social proof | "This one LinkedIn change increased profile views in 2 weeks." |
| Aspiration | "POV: your LinkedIn finally looks like someone they'd want to hire." |
| Skeptic | "Same person. Same face. Just professionally lit." |
| Career stats | "Why do less qualified people get more opportunities?" |

## Why don't our UGC videos look like ads?

Every format is borrowed from organic TikTok and Reels — question stickers, POV captions, trend memes, handwritten signs, a FaceTime call with your past self, a chat screen — and almost every one carries the proof: the real selfie next to the headshot. Every video ships in **9:16 and 4:5**.

Three rules: **native first · proof inside · real people in real rooms.**

## Does it matter who's in the video?

A lot. The same pain script ran at **0.45X** cost per customer with one creator and **0.85X** with the other (X = account average). We match each message to the person who sells it best — not to follower count.

## What makes a UGC ad script sell?

Short, personal, in the customer's own words, under 20 seconds.

| Openings that sold | Cost per customer | Openings we dropped | Cost per customer |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Interview in 2 days. No headshot." | 0.4X | "Recruiters judge you in 100 ms." | 0 sales |
| "Recruiters notice your photo first." | 0.45X | "Your career is not a coincidence." | 3.9X |
| "From my worst selfies to my best headshot." | 0.55X | "Does it actually look like you?" | 1.2X |
| "My LinkedIn views tripled." | 0.65X | Anything longer than 20 s | 1.25X |

Structure that worked: **hook (0–3 s) → cost of the problem (3–8 s) → fix + proof on screen (8–15 s) → speed (15–18 s) → soft CTA (18–20 s).**

## How do you know which ad creatives work?

Every video gets a verdict after each round — **do more, fix, or stop** — based on cost per customer, frequency and hold rate. Round 1: **71 produced, 44 launched, 14 winners (1 in 3)**; a winner brought customers at 0.7X or less.

What we learned:
- Voice-over beat talking-to-camera: **0.7X vs 0.95X** per customer.
- Short wins: under 20 s **0.85X**, longer **1.25X**.
- Showing the change beats talking about LinkedIn: **0.55X vs 1X**.

## Results

Measured on the client's real Meta account, where several agencies and in-house teams compete for budget:

- **0.65X** cost per customer for our best big-budget video;
- **#1 by budget among ~770 ads** — and the most sales;
- **0.88X** average across all round-1 videos — 12% below the account.

Round 2: five creators, ten new trend formats, ~63 videos, each script assigned to the creator who sells that message best.
