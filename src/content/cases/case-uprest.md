UpRest is a restaurant POS and back-office software brand that was ready to launch in Portugal. They had a strong product — free hardware, smart automation, and AI-powered food cost control — but no content, no validated messaging, no sales funnel, and no understanding of how the Portuguese market would respond.

![UpRest product interface and restaurant POS system dashboard](https://framerusercontent.com/images/XIYSupHDCblGZrlbaHyqUJItjCk.jpg?width=2800&height=1524)


## Objectives

Together, we defined four clear goals:

- **Launch in Portugal** with a clear, localized value proposition
- **Test and validate offers** to optimize for lowest cost per lead
- **Build a lead generation system** from scratch with lightweight tools
- **Scale with platform-native content** on Meta and LinkedIn


## Phase 1: Research and Offer Hypotheses

We started with rapid market analysis:

- **Target audience mapping** — restaurant owners and managers in Portugal
- **Pain point identification** — cost control, tech overwhelm, unreliable support
- **Value pillars** — free equipment, AI-based automation, simple setup and onboarding

From there, we built offer hypotheses to test across formats and platforms.

![UpRest market research findings and target audience segmentation](https://framerusercontent.com/images/iVHIlESzVHsvOS1ipDxvDfP9M.jpg?width=2800&height=1444)


## Phase 2: Full Marketing Kit from Scratch

With no existing content, we moved fast to build everything UpRest needed:

- **One-pager and pitch deck** — for structured lead nurturing
- **Ad copy and offer variants** — aligned with buyer pain points
- **Visuals, animations, and AI-generated creatives** — for ad campaigns
- **20+ posts for Meta and LinkedIn** — to fuel brand visibility

Everything was designed to be modular, testable, and scalable.


## Phase 3: Video Ads in 3-10 Days

To reach the audience fast, we produced 20-30 second video ads with a mobile-first, story-driven approach:

- **Scripting** — via Google Sheets + Figma templates
- **Filming** — with phones for native social feel
- **Editing** — via CapCut Pro and Adobe AI for voice/audio polish
- **Output** — optimized for Meta Ads (Stories and Reels)

Time-to-publish for most assets: under 120 minutes.

![UpRest video ad production storyboard and editing workflow](https://framerusercontent.com/images/eLGWmPc6osb4CQYvKUV6YnbE8.jpg?width=2800&height=1046)


## Phase 4: Campaign Launch and Optimization

We launched multiple campaign variations on Meta:

- **Testing** — different hooks, visuals, copy, and CTAs
- **Monitoring** — CPL, CTR, CVR tracked daily
- **Scaling** — winning combos amplified, underperformers cut
- **Iterating** — results reinvested into new creative tests

The feedback loop was weekly — not quarterly.


## Results

### CPL Improvement

- **Before:** EUR 10.97 per lead
- **After:** EUR 2.75 per lead

Achieved through creative testing, message clarity, and hyper-focused ad spend.

![UpRest campaign performance dashboard showing CPL drop and lead volume](https://framerusercontent.com/images/4QkvKkWCkVwWNotXRfxevWHJE.jpg?width=2800&height=1860)

### 100+ Qualified Leads

- **High-intent leads** on a lean budget
- **Precision targeting** — owners and decision-makers
- **Stable results** — client paused scaling by choice, not necessity

### Client Feedback

> "Leads are great. Great quality, truly interested leads."
> "Only one bad apple so far."
> "Cost stabilized, we're not increasing budgets for now."

### What Made It Work

- **Clear hypotheses** leading to structured testing
- **Agile video production** enabling fast learning cycles
- **Platform-native content** built specifically for Meta and LinkedIn
- **End-to-end managed process** — no internal marketing hires needed
