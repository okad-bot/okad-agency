Discover how UpRest launched in Portugal with zero assets and slashed cost per lead by over 3× using lean, platform-native B2B lead generation campaigns. Dive into our step-by-step approach—from rapid market insights and modular content builds to agile video ads and weekly optimization—that delivered 100+ qualified leads. Build your own growth engine now!

In B2B marketing, there’s one golden rule: if your offer doesn’t stop the scroll, you’re already dead. Brands that refuse to test, adapt, and produce native creatives fast enough don’t just fall behind — they vanish.

UpRest — a restaurant POS and back office software brand — was ready to launch in Portugal. They had a powerful product and a bold offer: free hardware, smart automation, and AI-powered food cost control.

But there was a problem.

They had no content.No validated messaging.No sales funnel.No understanding of how the Portuguese market would respond.

Their challenge was universal: how to enter a new market fast, build brand awareness, and generate high-quality leads — without wasting weeks and budget on guesswork.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/XIYSupHDCblGZrlbaHyqUJItjCk.jpg?width=2800&height=1524)


### What We Set Out to Do

Together, we defined four clear goals:

Launch in Portugal with a clear, localized value proposition

Test and validate offers to optimize for lowest cost per lead (CPL)

Build a lightweight but effective lead generation system from scratch

Scale with platform-native content on Meta and LinkedIn


### Phase 1: Research + Offer Hypotheses

We started with rapid market analysis, including:

Mapping the target audience: restaurant owners and managers

Identifying pain points (cost control, tech overwhelm, unreliable support)

Defining value pillars:

Free equipment

AI-based automation

Simple setup and onboarding

From there, we built offer hypotheses to test across formats and platforms.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/iVHIlESzVHsvOS1ipDxvDfP9M.jpg?width=2800&height=1444)


### Phase 2: Full Marketing Kit from Scratch

With no existing content, we moved fast to build everything UpRest needed:

🧩 One-pager & pitch deck: for structured lead nurturing

🎯 Ad copy & offer variants: aligned with buyer pain points

🎥 Visuals, animations & AI-generated creatives

📲 Over 20 posts for Meta & LinkedIn: to fuel brand visibility

Everything was designed to be modular, testable, and scalable.


### Phase 3: Video Ads in 3–10 Days

To reach our audience fast, we produced 20–30 second video ads with a mobile-first, story-driven approach:

Scripting via Google Sheets + Figma templates

Filming with phones for native social feel

Editing via CapCut Pro and Adobe AI for voice/audio polish

Output optimized for Meta Ads (Stories & Reels)

Time-to-publish for most assets: under 120 minutes.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/eLGWmPc6osb4CQYvKUV6YnbE8.jpg?width=2800&height=1046)


### Phase 4: Campaign Launch & Performance Optimization

We launched multiple campaign variations on Meta:

Testing different hooks, visuals, copy, and CTAs

Monitoring CPL, CTR, CVR daily

Scaling winning combos and cutting what underperformed

Reinvesting results into new creative tests

The feedback loop was weekly — not quarterly. Speed = learning.


### Results: CPL Cut by 3x + Predictable Lead Flow


### 🚀 CPL Improvement

Before: €10.97 per lead

We achieved this through creative testing, message clarity, and hyper-focused ad spend.

### Lessons for B2B Brands Launching in New Markets

If you're a SaaS, AdTech, or vertical-specific B2B platform entering a new region, here’s what you should take from this case:

Don’t wait for perfect. Get to market with the leanest version of your story — and start learning.

Use paid social to validate fast. Meta is still a goldmine for discovery-stage B2B leads — if you speak the language of the feed.

Creative > media budget. If your offer hits, you can lower costs and increase volume without spending more.

Test like a product team. Weekly sprints, fast feedback, and rapid iteration beat static campaigns every time.
