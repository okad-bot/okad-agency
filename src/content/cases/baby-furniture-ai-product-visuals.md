**Nursery ads without a nursery shoot.** Dadada Baby sells premium cribs and dressers through six channels — their own site, Amazon, Walmart and three wholesale partners. Each channel needs its own visuals: different sizes, different moods, different copy. A traditional shoot would mean renting a nursery, styling six setups and reshooting every time a new finish launches.

Instead, we took real photos customers had already posted — cribs in actual nurseries — and used AI to build polished lifestyle scenes around them. Same furniture, new rooms, new lighting, new models, all generated.

## What we delivered

- **40+ static posts and ads** — hero banners, carousel cards, comparison grids
- **6 sales channels covered** — each with format-specific crops and copy
- **0 product shoots** — every image built from existing customer photos
- AI-generated scenes matched to each channel's audience: warm and editorial for DTC, clean and spec-focused for marketplace listings

The result: a full visual library that looks like a multi-day shoot, produced in days, refreshable any time a new SKU drops.
