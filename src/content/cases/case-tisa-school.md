TISA is a private school offering the International Baccalaureate (IB) curriculum, with campuses in Leiden (Netherlands) and Lisbon (Portugal). The Dutch campus had government support for private education, but the Lisbon campus entered a tougher landscape — free public schools dominate, and tuition fees face scrutiny. TISA needed a full-scale digital marketing and lead generation engine.

## Objectives

- **Drive awareness and enrollment** for the Lisbon campus
- **Build social media presence** that reflects the school's character
- **Improve lead generation** through online campaigns and school events
- **Automate communications** through chatbots and email marketing
- **Redesign the website** for conversion optimization

## No In-House Marketing Team

The school had no internal marketing resources. Content creation, lead response, and digital strategy were virtually nonexistent. We stepped in as a full-stack marketing department and built a system for:

- **Daily content collection** — photos and updates via teacher WhatsApp chats
- **Content curation** — editing raw material into polished social media assets
- **Publishing management** — maintaining calendars and engagement loops

## Explaining the IB System Clearly

The IB program is rich and rigorous but hard to explain to parents used to local curricula. We invested time in learning the educational model so we could communicate its advantages clearly through posts, visuals, and on-site copy.

## Website Redesign for Conversion

The school's website lacked both structure and conversion logic. We completely redesigned the site with a hierarchy that guides users through:

- **What makes the school different** — IB curriculum, international environment
- **What outcomes families can expect** — academic results, university placement
- **How to book a tour or consultation** — clear CTAs and lead capture forms

We also added language toggles, optimized for mobile, and installed lead-tracking systems.

## Lead Management and Automation

When school staff were unavailable, no one followed up with incoming inquiries. We introduced:

- **Chatbots** — pre-programmed flows to answer common parent questions
- **Email automations** — sequences tailored to enrollment stages
- **Call scripts and lead management** — handled directly by our team

This prevented drop-offs during busy admissions periods.

## Results

- **Cost per qualified lead** — dropped to EUR 2-5 for a school with EUR 1,500/month tuition
- **100+ qualified leads** generated during the campaign period
- **Site conversion rate** increased significantly after the redesign
- **Automation** improved response time and kept leads warm
- **Consistent content** built trust and showcased real school life
