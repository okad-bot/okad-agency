UGC pricing is opaque. Creators do not know what to charge. Brands do not know what to pay. The result is a market where identical work is priced anywhere from $50 to $500 depending on who you ask.

This guide provides actual pricing data based on rates across 700+ active UGC creators. Use it to set fair rates whether you are hiring or pitching.

## Rates by Experience Level

| Tier | Per Video | Projects Completed | Revision Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry-level | $50–$150 | Under 20 | 30–50% |
| Mid-tier | $150–$300 | 50–200 | 10–20% |
| Expert | $300–$500+ | 200+ | Under 5% |

The revision rate column matters more than it appears. An entry-level creator at $75/video with a 40% revision rate has an effective cost of $105 per usable asset (accounting for time spent on feedback and re-shoots). A mid-tier creator at $200/video with a 10% revision rate has an effective cost of $220 per usable asset.

**Cost per usable asset** is the metric that matters, not cost per video.

## Rates by Niche

Specialized verticals command premiums:

| Niche | Range | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Beauty/skincare | $100–$250 | Abundant supply, competitive rates |
| Food/beverage | $75–$175 | High volume, lower barrier |
| Fashion | $100–$250 | Visual complexity, styling needed |
| Health/fitness | $125–$275 | Moderate supply, authenticity critical |
| SaaS/tech | $200–$400 | Screen recording expertise, product comprehension |
| Finance/fintech | $250–$500 | Compliance-sensitive, limited qualified pool |
| Alcohol/spirits | $300–$500+ | Age-gated, highly restricted creator pool |
| Healthcare/pharma | $300–$500+ | Regulatory requirements, legal review needed |

Regulated industries carry a 40–80% premium over consumer goods. The creator pool is smaller, compliance requirements are stricter, and the cost of getting content wrong (regulatory fines, legal liability) justifies higher rates. ## Rates by Deliverable Type

The scope of work significantly affects pricing:

| Deliverable | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Raw footage (no editing) | $50–$100 | Creator films, brand edits |
| Basic edited video | $100–$200 | Simple cuts, creator handles editing |
| Full ad creative | $200–$400 | Complete ad-ready asset with text overlays |
| Screen recording + voiceover | $150–$300 | Tech UGC format |
| Hook variation pack | +$25–$75 per hook | Additional opening variations for the same body |
| Whitelisting access | +$100–$300/month | Running ads from creator's account |
| Exclusivity | +25–50% | Creator cannot work with competitors |
| Usage rights extension | +$50–$200 | Beyond standard 30–90 day license |

## Four Pricing Models

### 1. Per-video pricing
Best for testing and small volumes. Highest unit cost but no commitment. Pay $100–$400 per finished video.

**When to use:** Starting UGC for the first time, testing a new niche, working with a creator for the first time.

### 2. Monthly retainer
8–20+ videos per month at a fixed rate. Saves 20–40% compared to per-video pricing. Guarantees creator availability and priority.

**When to use:** Consistent monthly UGC needs, proven creator relationship, predictable budget.

### 3. Bulk package
10–50+ videos at a discounted rate. Saves 30–50%. Commitment-based pricing.

**When to use:** Campaign-based work, seasonal pushes, building a content library.

### 4. Day rate
$500–$2,000 per day. Most cost-effective for volume shoots. A skilled creator produces 5–10 videos in a day session.

**When to use:** Large-scale production, multiple products to cover, building a modular creative library.

## Agency vs. Direct Hiring

The crossover point is approximately 10 videos per month.

**Under 10 videos/month:** Direct hiring may be cheaper in raw creator fees. But add the hidden costs: finding and vetting creators, managing briefs and deadlines, handling revisions, dealing with no-shows or poor quality. Your effective hourly rate on management likely exceeds the savings.

**Over 10 videos/month:** Agency services typically offer better total cost of ownership. Agencies absorb creator sourcing, quality control, revision management, and failed deliveries. The per-video rate is higher, but the cost per usable asset is often lower.

| Factor | Direct Hiring | Agency |
|---|---|---|
| Per-video cost | Lower ($75–$250) | Higher ($150–$400) |
| Management time | 2–5 hours/week | Minimal |
| Revision management | You handle | Agency handles |
| Failed deliveries | Your cost | Agency absorbs |
| Creator vetting | You do it | Pre-vetted network |
| Cost per usable asset | Often higher | Often lower |

## Negotiation Strategies for Brands

**Commit to volume.** "We need 15 videos per month ongoing" gets better rates than "Let's try one video." Creators value predictability over premium one-offs.

**Bundle hook variations.** Instead of paying full price per variation, negotiate a package: "Base video + 3 hook variations for [rate]." Additional hooks cost the creator 10 minutes of work, not a full production.

**Simplify briefs.** Complex briefs with multiple scenes, specific locations, and detailed wardrobe requirements cost more. Streamlined briefs (talking head, one product, natural setting) enable lower rates.

**Offer faster payment.** Net-7 or immediate payment gets better rates than Net-30. Creators deal with cash flow uncertainty — fast payment has real value.

**Waive exclusivity.** Exclusivity adds 25–50% to the cost. Unless you genuinely need category exclusivity, let the creator work with other brands. Most brands do not need it.

## Negotiation Strategies for Creators

**Track your metrics.** If your content consistently delivers hook rates above 50% or CPA below the brand's target, your rates should reflect that. Data-backed rate increases are easier to justify than "I want more."

**Offer packages, not single videos.** "5 videos for $X" sounds like a better deal than "$Y per video" even at the same effective rate. Packages signal professionalism and reduce the brand's decision-making friction.

**Set minimums.** A minimum project size ($300–$500) filters out clients who want five videos for $50 each. Your time has a floor value — protect it.

**Add usage tiers.** Base rate for organic use, premium rate for paid advertising, additional fee for whitelisting. This aligns your compensation with the value the brand extracts from your content.

## Budget Recommendations

| Stage | Monthly Budget | Volume | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Testing | $2,000–$5,000 | 10–25 videos | Find winning formats and creators |
| Scaling | $5,000–$15,000 | 25–60+ videos | Feed ad campaigns with fresh creative |
| Mature | $10,000–$30,000+ | 60–150+ videos | Continuous pipeline with creator network |

Start with testing-level budgets. Prove ROI on 10–25 videos before committing to scale.
