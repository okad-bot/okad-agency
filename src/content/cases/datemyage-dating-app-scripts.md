**Same faces. New words.** The brand's best ads kept featuring the same 22 creators. Instead of finding new people, I wrote 24 new scripts and laid them over the footage that already worked. Each script was cut for this app and its sister app.

## What I delivered

- **24 new scripts** for existing creator footage
- **48 videos** in the plan (each script cut for two apps)
- **22 creators** reused from winning ads
- **7 scripts** with AI voiceover
