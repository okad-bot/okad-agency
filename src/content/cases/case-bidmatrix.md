## Modern Wix Website and Brand Identity for a B2B Platform Delivered in One Cycle

Discover how Bit Matrix escaped the AdTech cookie-cutter trap with a unique brand language, modular dark-mode Wix website, and sales-ready media deck—all built with no-code agility. Our step-by-step process created a cohesive, scalable design system that boosts trust, memorability, and conversions. Ready to stand out? Let’s build your brand.

April 16, 2025

In a market where every tech company looks like a clone of the next, the brands that win are the ones that dare to design their own language.

Bit Matrix is a B2B technology platform that manages advertising integrations within mobile applications. It operates in a space where technical reliability matters — but so does trust, memorability, and perceived value. Bit Matrix came to us with a clear challenge:

"We like what our competitors are doing, but we don’t want to look like them."

This paradox is common among SaaS and AdTech brands. Founders often admire competitor aesthetics, but they intuitively know differentiation is the key to growth — especially when pitching to new clients or partners.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/qaDRH3v2sUcWF4m24yLZzhfO0s.jpg?width=4200&height=3340)


### Project Scope

Our collaboration with Bit Matrix included:

Full brand development, including visual identity and tone of voice

Creation of a media deck to support sales and partnership efforts

Design and development of a no-code website with responsive, dark-theme UX

Design of social media templates to ensure consistency across channels


### Designing for a Tech Brand That Needed to Look Smarter Than the Rest


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/14YWe9pflppRvsRtfbLbvdlEK9w.jpg?width=4200&height=5233)


### 1. Brand Direction & Custom Visual Elements

The initial direction was influenced by a competitor's visual style. Our job was to take that energy — and turn it into something entirely original.

We tested various layout systems and typographic structures

We explored graphic forms that suggested tech, scale, and control

We built a proprietary set of graphic elements, fully unique to Bit Matrix

The final design language positioned Bit Matrix as confident, modern, and distinctly not another cookie-cutter AdTech brand.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/UXiT28d6uJWq5TjNrNzctiLT2A.jpg?width=4200&height=3766)


### 2. Color & Contrast Strategy

The brand identity uses a dark foundation with three strong accent colors

This not only provides visual impact but enhances accessibility and modularity

It’s a scalable system — easy to apply across website, media materials, and social content


### The Website: Built on No-Code, Designed to Scale

To launch quickly without sacrificing quality, we chose Wix (a no-code platform) to develop the Bit Matrix site.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/d3IzPxxljDa4sYlA5TPHzQG5xSM.jpg?width=4200&height=3115)


### Key UX/UI Decisions:

A fixed, grid-based structure that works across desktop and mobile

A dark UI with high-contrast highlights for a sleek, tech-forward feel

Modular design elements that reflect product logic and hierarchy

The result is a professional, lightweight, and responsive site that delivers clarity, confidence, and conversion — all without code.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/eKVMxXsnGco47oqs5Z3EdlQQ3PE.jpg?width=4200&height=6880)


### The Media Deck: A Lighter Approach to Serious Business

While the site focused on boldness and energy, the media deck needed to present Bit Matrix in a calmer, more readable format.

We used light backgrounds and simplified typography

The layout guides the reader smoothly through company positioning, features, and benefits

Visual elements mirrored the website style for a seamless brand experience

Together, the site and media deck created a unified brand ecosystem — one that worked in both public and private channels.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/QyiekyOj8eub5OiTNWcVh36GdnA.jpg?width=4200&height=4180)


### Project Results

A cohesive, scalable brand identity for digital and offline use

A high-converting website that clearly communicates value

A sales-ready media deck that simplifies the pitch process

A design system that differentiates Bit Matrix from lookalike competitors

Positive client feedback and referrals to other tech companies


### What Other B2B Tech Companies Can Learn From This


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/YvTUT5D8Hv3IdDbT4Y5HSPwp8.jpg?width=4200&height=3547)

Whether you’re in AdTech, SaaS, or mobile infrastructure — the lesson is clear:

You don’t need to look like your competitors to be taken seriously.Unique design, when done well, builds more trust, not less.

Dark-mode websites can still be clean and businesslike.The key is hierarchy, legibility, and intelligent color use.

Your media deck is a sales tool — not a PDF placeholder.Design it with as much care as your homepage.

No-code ≠ low quality.With the right design system, you can launch fast and look premium.
