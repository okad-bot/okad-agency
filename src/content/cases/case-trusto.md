Trusto is a startup building chargeback prevention solutions for online businesses. They needed a pitch deck to attract investors and a website to convert potential clients — both requiring clear communication of a technically complex product.

![Trusto brand overview and product positioning](https://framerusercontent.com/images/DQReAx81BL5s8eefcRFdKnfnvk4.png?width=1400&height=455)

## Redesigning the Pitch Deck

The pitch deck was Trusto's first point of contact with investors. We rebuilt it from the ground up to tell a clear, persuasive story.

![Trusto pitch deck slide layout with structured sections](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Yaj1rRFaj7U6gmEMxByxkoK1nwA.png?width=1400&height=1361)

- **Logical Structure** — We restructured the presentation to take investors through a cohesive journey from the problem of chargebacks to Trusto's solution. Each slide addressed potential investor questions with clear, concise messaging.
- **Data Visualization** — We visualized key statistics showing Trusto's potential impact using charts, graphs, and infographics. These highlighted business savings and chargeback reduction numbers.
- **Typography and Color Choices** — High-contrast fonts paired with a palette of deep blues and light shades to convey professionalism, technology, and reliability.
- **Custom Visuals** — Instead of stock images, we designed unique icons and process diagrams explaining how Trusto works, making complex concepts accessible to all stakeholders.

![Trusto pitch deck data visualization slides](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Ax6R1fPdELWLRHrHAJFKMPLQ8.png?width=1400&height=1953)

![Trusto pitch deck typography and color system](https://framerusercontent.com/images/zpXARfvDWpCYJzNLOJIEue9B7e0.png?width=1400&height=1702)

## Building the Website

After the pitch deck, we turned to Trusto's public-facing website. The goal: make it as clear and engaging as the deck, giving potential clients an intuitive experience.

![Trusto website homepage design with value proposition and key benefits](https://framerusercontent.com/images/LxiXwGc0KzLGEm0Xdwf7X1PF8RA.png?width=1400&height=1906)

- **Clear Structure** — The homepage immediately answers three questions: What is Trusto? How does it work? What's in it for the user? A concise value proposition sits above the fold with visualized benefits below.
- **Simplified Illustrations and Diagrams** — We broke down the chargeback prevention process into step-by-step diagrams. Even non-technical visitors could grasp how Trusto benefits their business.
- **Micro-Animations** — Interactive animations in the "How It Works" section made the site feel dynamic and engaging, showcasing the prevention process in real time.
- **Minimalist Design** — Content takes center stage, with subtle gradients and small accents enhancing the technological feel.

![Trusto website section showing simplified process diagrams](https://framerusercontent.com/images/xxIQwXxGy5JKTHzTiqxTRtIrdkQ.png?width=1400&height=1296)

## Building Trust Without Case Studies

At launch, Trusto had no customer reviews or case studies. We focused on establishing credibility through other means:

- **Transparency** — Detailed sections explaining features and benefits with data-backed claims
- **Competitor Comparison** — Clear visualizations showing how Trusto stands out, emphasizing unique advantages
- **Minimalist Yet Warm Style** — Professional and tech-focused, but approachable rather than cold

![Trusto website competitor comparison and feature detail sections](https://framerusercontent.com/images/TV3etEKxfq5f7cdHcFq5HOSzMI.png?width=1400&height=1930)

We tested several homepage iterations to find the best balance between messaging, value propositions, and visual focus — maximizing clarity while drawing users in.

## Results

The pitch deck and website became essential tools for Trusto to establish credibility in the chargeback prevention market. Clear messaging, strong visuals, and minimalist design helped Trusto confidently attract both investors and clients in a competitive industry.
