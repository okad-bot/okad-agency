**Thirty ads a day. From one library.** A Swiss skincare brand had hundreds of UGC clips in its Drive and needed fresh ads every single day. I turned that library into a production line: about 10 new videos a day, three hooks each, so roughly 30 ads ready to test daily. Orders stayed stable for two to three months straight.

## What I delivered

- **~30 ads a day** at peak
- **10+ new videos a day**, 3+ hooks per video
- **2–3 months** of stable orders
- Competitor research, scripts, AI voiceover, editing
- Hook swap system — weak hooks replaced without touching the body
