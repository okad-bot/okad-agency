**5,471 creators asked in. Zero new shoots.** For our own brand account @by.okad, we wanted to prove a point: you can build a content engine from footage you already have. No new shoots, no creator bookings, no ad budget — just clips from the camera roll, screen recordings and behind-the-scenes moments.

We published Reels about working with UGC creators, running a creative agency and hiring tech creators — all cut from existing footage.

## What we delivered

- **81.8K views** on the best-performing Reel
- **$0 ad spend** — every view organic
- Content built entirely from existing camera-roll clips and screen recordings
- A system that turns everyday agency moments into publishable Reels

The Reel that hit 81.8K wasn't polished — it was a quick behind-the-scenes clip with a bold text hook. Production value mattered less than the hook and the topic.
