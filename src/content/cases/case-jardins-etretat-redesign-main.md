Les Jardins d'Etretat is an artistic venue in France known for events, cultural programming, and its atmospheric garden. Their existing website did not reflect the quality of the physical experience, and they needed a modern, intuitive digital presence that would boost ticket sales and serve a diverse audience — including older and non-tech-savvy visitors.

## Project Goals

- **Convey the space's identity** — softness, creativity, and flow — through design
- **Prioritize usability** for all audiences, including older visitors
- **Empower the client** to manage and update content independently
- **Create a timeless design** that works on all devices

## Interactive Map as Navigation

![Les Jardins d'Etretat website homepage with interactive venue map](https://framerusercontent.com/images/X6wQvxa7l1pxur24zEDoJYCLUpQ.jpg?width=2800&height=2280)

We created a fully interactive map of the venue. Visitors can hover over key areas, click through to event pages, and explore the space online before arriving in person.

## Dynamic Image Gallery

Inspired by the organic atmosphere of the garden and studio spaces, we built a free-form image gallery. Images vary in size and placement, creating visual rhythm that mimics the spontaneous beauty of the real space.

## Organic Visual Language

To reflect the venue's identity, we incorporated:

- **Curved backgrounds** and flowing lines
- **Rounded buttons** and UI elements
- **Warm, nature-inspired color palette**

These elements create a sense of calm and creativity throughout the site.

![Les Jardins d'Etretat organic design language and typography](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Q88HvkANMtdsrJFS15GlOJqAg8.jpg?width=2800&height=1575)

## Typography Curation

We tested over 12 font combinations to strike the right balance between legibility and elegance. The final choice communicates creativity without compromising accessibility — essential for a broad and diverse audience.

## Built on Framer for Flexibility

We chose Framer for its clean visual interface, low-code environment, and built-in design responsiveness. By minimizing scripts and relying on native Framer functionality, we reduced maintenance costs while keeping the site fast and scalable.

## Client Training and Empowerment

To future-proof the solution, we recorded tutorial videos in both French and English, covering:

- **Editing** dynamic content blocks
- **Managing** event schedules
- **Publishing** multilingual pages
- **Using** Framer's component system for independent layout adjustments

![Les Jardins d'Etretat full website page designs across sections](https://framerusercontent.com/images/8pDRf7lxiKY8wPhuh1tM5KYFJsk.jpg?width=2800&height=9596)

## UX and Structure Enhancements

- **Minimalist homepage:** focused on venue introduction, ticket purchase CTA, and current events
- **Streamlined navigation:** simplified information architecture, flattened menus, improved discoverability
- **Audience-centric layouts:** intuitive for older users, avoiding overly experimental interactions

## Results

- **Aesthetic upgrade** that communicates the venue's personality through design
- **Improved usability** across all devices and demographics
- **Lower maintenance costs** thanks to Framer's built-in tools
- **Easy content management** by the internal team without developer support
- **Delivered in under 2 months** from project start to launch
