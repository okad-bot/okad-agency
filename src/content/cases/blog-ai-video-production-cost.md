Every agency offering AI video in 2026 gives you a different price. Some quote per minute, some per project, some per month. The ranges are so wide — $500 to $50,000 — that the numbers are useless without context.

Here are real costs from three different AI video project types we delivered between February and October 2026. Not estimates, not ranges pulled from industry reports — actual production costs broken down by what was included and what the client received.

## How AI Video Production Actually Works

Before the pricing makes sense, you need to understand the process. AI video production in 2026 is not "type a prompt, get a video." That approach produces generic, unusable content.

The method that produces broadcast-quality results: hand-compose key frames, then use AI to animate between them. Tools like Seedance generate 4-6 second clips from carefully directed starting and ending frames. The creative team decides every composition, every camera angle, every lighting choice. AI handles the motion.

The principle is simple: **AI never decides the frame.** A human art director composes every key moment. AI fills the gaps between those moments with fluid animation. This is why professionally directed AI video looks fundamentally different from AI video generated by prompts alone.

- Session cost: under $700

At $700 per session versus $3,000-8,000 for a traditional product shoot producing comparable volume, this tier makes economic sense for any brand publishing across multiple channels. The same system, adapted for Godka (an alcohol brand), generated cocktail visuals that drove 200K-900K organic views per post.

This is where AI video production replaces or supplements an in-house content team. A brand gets a set number of posts and videos per month, produced on a rolling schedule, with creative direction aligned to their brand system.

The economics here: this brand was spending on monthly shoots in Paris to maintain their content calendar. AI production replaced those shoots entirely while increasing output volume. The monthly package cost was a fraction of a single shoot day plus travel.

## What Drives AI Video Cost Up or Down

**Costs less:**
- Using existing brand assets as source material (vs creating from scratch)
- Consistent visual style across multiple pieces (brand system already established)
- Shorter clips (4-6 seconds vs 30-60 seconds)
- Fewer revision cycles (clear brief = fewer iterations)

**Costs more:**
- Character animation (vs product/object animation)
- Multi-scene narratives (vs single-scene loops)
- New visual style development (first project with a brand always costs more than subsequent ones)
- Tight deadlines (rush fees apply, same as traditional)

## How to Evaluate AI Video Production Pricing

When comparing quotes from different agencies or freelancers, ask these questions:

1. **Who composes the key frames?** If the answer is "AI generates them from prompts," expect lower quality. Human-composed key frames are the difference between content that looks AI-generated and content that looks intentional.

2. **How many revision cycles are included?** Two cycles is standard. Unlimited revisions is a red flag — it means the process is not structured enough to get close on the first pass.

3. **What is the per-unit breakdown?** Monthly packages should break down to a per-piece cost. If the math does not work out to a reasonable per-unit rate, the package is overpriced.

4. **What source material do they need from you?** Agencies that can work with customer photos and existing assets save you the cost of creating source material. Agencies that require professional photography as input add a hidden cost.

5. **What is the turnaround for revisions?** 24-48 hours for minor revisions is standard. Anything longer suggests the workflow is not optimized.
