**800K views on one Reel. Shot as b-roll.** Your Christian Date is a dating app for Christians. Instead of buying finished UGC videos one by one, I ordered raw footage from creators: one-liners to camera and a library of everyday b-roll. My editors turned that footage into 60 videos a month. Then I shot one special series, a first-timer in church, and it became the account's biggest hit.
