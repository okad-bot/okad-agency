**68 ads. Story before product.** A European brand launched a daily longevity drink for people 40 and older. They had a launch film, a nutrition scientist on camera and a stack of creator clips. I turned that footage into a library of ads built on three things: a scientist who explains, real people with real stories, and AI b-roll and charts that show what happens inside the body. The product never shows up before the story has done its job.
