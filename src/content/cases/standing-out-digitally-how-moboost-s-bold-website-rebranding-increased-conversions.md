Imagine having a powerful product that revolutionizes cross-platform advertising but being held back by an outdated website. This was precisely the challenge Moboost—a data-driven advertising platform trusted by global brands like KFC, Adidas, Binance, HBO, and Inter—faced when they approached OKAD Agency. Here's the story of how a strategic, vibrant rebranding dramatically increased their digital visibility and boosted conversions.


![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/RoErPlMGOAo9XuawoTmr8dW3ntE.jpg?width=1400&height=874)


### Recognizing the Challenge

Moboost's original website wasn't effectively communicating its value or standing out in the competitive digital advertising landscape. The website felt generic, lacked visual impact, and didn't highlight key product advantages, resulting in missed opportunities and lower conversion rates.


### Crafting a Bold, Memorable Identity

Our first step was creating a visually striking and memorable design. We tested various color schemes and visual styles, ultimately choosing vibrant gradients and bold colors to differentiate Moboost from its competitors. These dynamic visuals captured attention immediately and communicated the innovative, energetic essence of the brand.

The block-based structure of the new design not only provided visual clarity but also ensured easy navigation, guiding users smoothly through the information funnel toward conversion points.


### Clarifying Content for Better Conversions

Working closely with Moboost, we refined website content to clearly articulate key product advantages. By focusing on concise, impactful messaging, we ensured visitors could quickly grasp the benefits of Moboost’s cross-platform advertising solutions, significantly enhancing the overall user experience and driving user actions.


### Optimizing for Global Reach

Recognizing Moboost's international clientele, our team ensured that the new website seamlessly operated across global markets. Special attention was given to technical optimization for complex markets such as China, where website accessibility without VPNs can be challenging. By implementing targeted technical solutions and optimizing loading speeds, we provided an effortless browsing experience worldwide.


### Real Results: Increased Visibility and Conversions

The impact was immediate and tangible. Moboost’s redesigned website saw significantly increased engagement, longer user sessions, and, crucially, higher conversion rates. The bold visuals and clear content structure effectively translated visitors into leads and inquiries, enhancing the overall performance and profitability of the business.

Client feedback was overwhelmingly positive, highlighting the ease of navigation, improved user experience, and striking visual design as key factors in their enhanced online presence.
