**Red flags first. The jar later.** A US gut health brand had creator testimonials and product shots, and every ad opened on the jar. I rebuilt them around things people feel but don't talk about, then let AI b-roll explain what's happening inside the body. The product comes last.

## What I delivered

- **~20 edits a month**
- **8 AI b-roll clips** made for the scripts
- **4 story formats** — red flags, gut-brain connection, week-by-week progress, gift story
- Zero new shoots — all from existing creator footage
