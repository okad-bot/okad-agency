SUNC MSU (Specialized Educational and Scientific Center of Moscow State University) is a selective school for talented students. The alumni association needed merchandise that would serve as a symbol of membership, a reward for community engagement, and a tool for fostering connection among graduates.

![SUNC MSU alumni community branding elements and the iconic cube symbol](https://framerusercontent.com/images/ObNxOzeSwg8itqxeLTXxQKeAbbk.jpg?width=2800&height=1948)

## The Challenge

The project had specific constraints:

- **No detailed brief** — the client preferred to see design concepts immediately rather than write a spec
- **Younger demographic** — previous merch targeted alumni aged 45+, but this collection needed to appeal to the 18-35 range
- **Dual purpose** — the merch had to work as both a symbol of belonging and an earned reward for participation in alumni activities

## Design Approach

Instead of waiting for a traditional brief, we created three concepts to present in a single call, with each designer on the team proposing a distinct direction.

![Three design concept explorations based on the SUNC cube symbol](https://framerusercontent.com/images/lS4NIfg6lnXMQltHgSsXKLozgiE.jpg?width=2800&height=1686)

## Three Concepts

All three concepts built on the community's symbol — the cube — and aligned with alumni values:

![Detailed mockups of all three merch concepts showing t-shirt, hoodie, and accessory applications](https://framerusercontent.com/images/TYd4frYI4S2Fbh6pImz5xNLPrBk.jpg?width=2800&height=3356)

- **NFT Cube** — digital art-inspired, modern and unique, targeting digitally-oriented younger alumni
- **Programmer's Style** — monospace fonts, code-style layout, vectorized cube mimicking stylized code, aimed at tech-savvy graduates
- **Classic University Style** — clean lines, minimalism, and traditional university aesthetic. Simple enough for mass production while capturing community identity

## The Chosen Direction

The client selected the classic university style for its versatility and production friendliness. We finalized the design by:

- Experimenting with font variations and typography
- Selecting color options for the t-shirt range
- Creating detailed mockups showing the final product in real-world context

![Final classic university style merch design with color variations and font options](https://framerusercontent.com/images/j0OlMz1xUPjU5wKS5v07T8H4E.jpg?width=2800&height=3800)

## Results

![Final SUNC MSU merchandise collection displayed on models and product photography](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Zwp2OlxDdJGo7sG0mFdsQrdfjq8.jpg?width=2800&height=4040)

- **Positive community reception** — alumni feedback confirmed the design resonated with the target 18-35 demographic
- **Fast delivery** — the multi-concept approach allowed rapid iteration without extended briefing cycles
- **Production-ready output** — minimalist design was easy to adapt for printing across different merchandise types
- **Authentic connection** — team members with SUNC alumni ties ensured the design reflected genuine community culture
