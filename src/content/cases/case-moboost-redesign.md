Moboost is a data-driven advertising platform built for cross-platform products — from television and mobile apps to desktop websites and connected digital environments. With clients including KFC, Adidas, Binance, HBO, and Inter, the platform helps brands scale reach, acquire users, and optimize ad performance across markets.

![Moboost client logos and platform overview showing cross-platform advertising capabilities](https://framerusercontent.com/images/vGomKO07iAihJyILqwrsVSZDsS4.jpg?width=2800&height=748)

## The Challenge

![Moboost original website showing dated design and UX issues](https://framerusercontent.com/images/dnV0uBYThEyZJDpUWWvOShWD38Y.jpg?width=2800&height=1748)

Moboost came to us with three goals:

- **Modernize the website** — make it more memorable, modern, and user-friendly
- **Highlight product advantages** — increase inquiries and conversions
- **Optimize for international markets** — especially Europe and China, including VPN-free access and technical localization

![Moboost product features and advertising technology ecosystem breakdown](https://framerusercontent.com/images/YdUD5zDhpTiRtNB5XtRGHthCI.jpg?width=2800&height=1748)

## Timeline: Full Transformation in 30 Days

The entire project was completed within one month:

- **Modular, scalable design system** — built for easy updates as the product evolves
- **Fully responsive interface** — tested across devices and screen sizes
- **Global performance optimization** — including China-specific infrastructure work

## Phase 1: UX & Visual Identity Overhaul

![Moboost design system color palette and typography specifications](https://framerusercontent.com/images/MzkJXcVCGssX8d7qloQ7zaP1r2g.jpg?width=2800&height=448)

We designed a clean yet striking interface on a block-based layout:

- **Bright gradients and vibrant accents** — cutting through the AdTech visual noise
- **Balanced creativity and professionalism** — appealing to enterprise clients
- **Collaborative iteration** — multiple visual directions tested with the Moboost team

![Full-page website design showing complete Moboost homepage and product sections](https://framerusercontent.com/images/9cye46Mt7BfArZafJackDMG7Q.jpg?width=2800&height=2760)

## Phase 2: Content Restructuring

We revised and simplified the content architecture together with the Moboost team:

- **Clear hierarchy and messaging** — product benefits front and center
- **Localized tone** — language flexibility built in for future translations
- **Audience-aware structure** — technical buyers, CMOs, and local agencies could all understand the offering within seconds

![Moboost website inner pages with product details, case studies, and partner sections](https://framerusercontent.com/images/MeiQm9mU3W4Gj0UmnkTGTTRUPo.jpg?width=2800&height=5710)

## Phase 3: China-Focused International Optimization

A major priority was ensuring reliable, fast access in China without VPN:

- **Infrastructure optimization** for Chinese accessibility
- **Lightweight assets** with adaptive content loading and backup protocols
- **Clean CMS structure** to support further international scaling

## Results

Within weeks of launch, Moboost had a fast, conversion-optimized website ready for international traffic:

- **Higher on-site engagement** across all key markets
- **Lower bounce rate** in target regions
- **Visible differentiation** in a crowded AdTech space — vibrant gradients and modular layout became instantly recognizable
