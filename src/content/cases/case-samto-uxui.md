Samto is a mental wellness app that helps users improve their emotional state through mood tracking, interactive tools, and an AI-powered assistant. The founding team needed more than UI screens — they needed UX strategy, visual identity, and iterative product thinking to go from concept to a scalable product.

## The Challenge

![Samto app initial wireframes and user flow diagrams for MVP planning](https://framerusercontent.com/images/aZuRx2DobB5yPL1RkIN2U6Gdqsg.jpg?width=2800&height=1390)

When the Samto team came to us, they were developing their MVP and needed help turning early product hypotheses into a coherent experience:

- **Feature prioritization** — Understanding what matters most to users
- **Onboarding and retention** — Building an intuitive structure that creates daily habits
- **Emotional branding** — Creating a brand that resonates in the mental health space


## Market Research and UX Hypotheses

![Samto competitive analysis of leading mental wellness and self-help apps](https://framerusercontent.com/images/2mFfRsLUfTb2gzLBkYantiPNUmw.jpg?width=2800&height=1860)

Before designing anything, we conducted deep competitive and user research:

- **Pattern analysis** — Studied UX patterns in leading mental wellness and self-help apps
- **Pain point mapping** — Identified key triggers users experience in onboarding and mood-tracking tools
- **Feature prioritization** — Proposed MVP features based on user needs and feasibility
- **Flexible architecture** — Designed an app structure allowing new features to integrate later


## Branding That Feels Like Emotional Support

![Samto brand identity with gradients, light effects, and energy core visual metaphor](https://framerusercontent.com/images/oqDwobjBagnAAzRLdVuAA8B8QMU.jpg?width=2800&height=1390)

In wellness tech, trust and tone matter as much as functionality. We crafted a visual identity that:

- **Communicates calm and safety** through soft gradients and light effects
- **Evokes mental recharge** using energy visuals and warm color transitions
- **Centers on a glowing energy core** as the visual metaphor for the AI assistant


## UX Design Rooted in Real User Flows

![Samto customer journey map from first app open to regular mood tracking](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Ys5Hf3eQXLlJjFmY0S2VLovBo50.jpg?width=2800&height=1860)

We mapped the entire user journey from download to daily habit:

- **Customer Journey Map** — From first app open to regular mood tracking
- **Friction point identification** — Designed around every potential drop-off
- **Optimized User Flow** — Clarity and engagement across the entire app experience
- **Competitor benchmarking** — Found emotional tone and onboarding clarity to be the biggest differentiators


## UI Design with Motion and Emotion

![Samto app UI screens showing AI assistant energy orb and mood tracking interface](https://framerusercontent.com/images/vHX9KmmG5JmmHgiA3sqkC53eXFg.jpg?width=2800&height=1860)

We translated UX flows into a fully interactive interface:

- **Light-filled gradients** with smooth animations and soft edge interactions
- **AI assistant as energy orb** that visually responds to user needs
- **Accessible typography and layout** designed for calmness and readability


## Website for Market Education and Conversion

![Samto marketing website design with app store download CTAs and feature highlights](https://framerusercontent.com/images/HpRSmAFYYdCzzNSiOuIBNJ5sgFc.jpg?width=2800&height=1860)

To support growth and acquisition, we designed the marketing site with:

- **Brand-aligned messaging** about the app's benefits
- **Trust-building design** based on user psychology
- **Flexible landing structure** supporting future A/B testing and content campaigns


## V2 Optimization Based on Real User Feedback

One year after launch, the Samto team returned for improvements. We:

- **Analyzed behavioral data** from early users
- **Improved navigation** and added user-requested features
- **Conducted iterative user testing** to validate UX updates


## Results

- **Unique visual identity** that sets Samto apart in the crowded wellness market
- **User-centered app flow** tested through real usage patterns
- **Marketing website** that effectively communicates benefits and drives downloads
- **Optimized V2** based on direct user feedback
- **Scalable design system** allowing future features to integrate easily
