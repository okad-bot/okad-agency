Every brand needs creative. Nobody knows what to pay for it. Agency pricing is deliberately opaque — most agencies do not publish rates because pricing varies by scope, and because opacity lets them charge more.

This guide fixes that. Here is what creative agencies actually charge in 2026, broken down by service type, engagement model, and brand size.

## Per-Unit Pricing: What Each Service Costs

### Ad Creative Production

| Service | Price Range | What You Get |
|---|---|---|
| Static ad (design + copy) | $100-$300 | One finished static ad with copy |
| Carousel ad | $200-$500 | 3-10 slides with design and copy |
| UGC video (standard) | $150-$500 | One creator, scripted, edited |
| UGC video (premium) | $500-$1,500 | Multi-creator, produced, multiple cuts |
| Hook swap edit | $15-$50 | New first 3 seconds on existing ad |
| Ad re-edit (existing footage) | $50-$150 | New version from existing assets |
| AI product photo | $15-$50 | One AI-generated lifestyle image |
| AI video ad | $100-$500 | AI-generated video with editing |

These are per-unit costs. Most agencies offer volume discounts at 10+ units per month.

OKAD's [Hook Swap System](/cases/swiss-skincare-high-volume-ads/) for a Swiss skincare brand kept costs at $15-$30 per new hook variant — systematically replacing weak openings while keeping proven body content intact. At that price point, testing 10 hook variations costs less than producing one new ad from scratch.

### Strategy Services

| Service | Price Range | What You Get |
|---|---|---|
| Creative audit | $500-$2,000 | Analysis of current ad performance + recommendations |
| Testing roadmap | $1,000-$3,000 | Structured plan for what to test and when |
| Monthly creative strategy | $2,000-$5,000 | Ongoing brief writing, performance analysis, iteration planning |
| Competitor analysis | $500-$1,500 | Deep dive on competitor ad strategy |

Strategy is the service most brands skip and most agencies upsell. But strategy without production is a document nobody acts on, and production without strategy is expensive guesswork.

## Monthly Retainer Models

| Retainer Tier | Monthly Cost | Typical Output | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $3,000-$5,000 | 5-10 ad concepts, basic strategy | Brands spending $5K-$15K/month on ads |
| Growth | $5,000-$10,000 | 15-25 ad concepts, full strategy | Brands spending $15K-$50K/month on ads |
| Scale | $10,000-$20,000 | 30-50+ concepts, strategy + testing | Brands spending $50K-$200K/month on ads |
| Enterprise | $20,000-$40,000+ | High-volume production + dedicated team | Brands spending $200K+/month on ads |

Retainers offer the best per-unit economics because the agency can plan resources efficiently. The trade-off is commitment — most retainers require 3-month minimums.

When [DateMyAge needed 48 videos](/cases/datemyage-dating-app-scripts/) from 24 scripts across 22 creators — including 7 with AI voiceover — the retainer model made the project possible. That volume through per-unit pricing would have cost 40-60% more because of the coordination overhead.

## Results-Based Pricing (RaaS)

The newest model ties agency compensation to outcomes rather than outputs. You pay per result — per conversion, per ROAS threshold met, or per performance milestone.

| RaaS Model | How It Works | Risk Profile |
|---|---|---|
| Per-deliverable | Pay per finished ad concept | Low risk — you know the cost upfront |
| Performance bonus | Base retainer + bonus tied to CPA/ROAS targets | Medium risk — shared upside and downside |
| Pure performance | Pay only when ads hit defined KPIs | High risk for agency — reserved for proven relationships |

RaaS works best when both sides have clear, measurable goals. For a deeper dive on this model, see our [RaaS vs retainer comparison](/blog/blog-raas-vs-retainer/) and our [RaaS pricing models guide](/blog/blog-raas-pricing-models/).

## Freelancer vs Agency vs In-House: Total Cost Comparison

| Factor | Freelancers | Agency | In-House Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly creative cost | $2,000-$6,000 | $5,000-$15,000 | $15,000-$30,000 |
| Strategy included | No | Yes (mid-tier+) | Yes |
| Production included | Partial — you manage | Yes | Yes |
| Coordination overhead | 10-15 hrs/week (your time) | Included | Included |
| Hidden cost of coordination | $30K-$50K/year | $0 | $0 |
| True annual cost | $54K-$122K | $60K-$180K | $180K-$360K |
| Ramp-up time | 1-2 weeks per freelancer | 2-3 weeks | 2-3 months (hiring) |
| Creative velocity | 5-15 concepts/month | 15-50+ concepts/month | 15-40 concepts/month |

The "true annual cost" row is where the comparison gets real. Freelancers look cheap until you add the 10-15 hours per week of internal coordination. In-house looks expensive until you factor in the creative velocity a dedicated team provides at scale.

For brands producing [80 ads from existing footage across five brands](/cases/ad-re-edits-five-brands/), the agency model delivered creative volume that would have required 3-4 internal hires — at a fraction of the annual cost.

## How to Budget Creative Relative to Ad Spend

The rule of thumb: spend 10-20% of your monthly ad spend on creative production.

| Monthly Ad Spend | Creative Budget | What That Buys |
|---|---|---|
| $5K-$10K | $500-$2,000 | 3-8 concepts, basic testing |
| $10K-$30K | $2,000-$6,000 | 10-20 concepts, structured testing |
| $30K-$100K | $5,000-$15,000 | 20-40 concepts, full strategy + testing |
| $100K-$300K | $15,000-$30,000 | 40-80 concepts, dedicated team |
| $300K+ | $30,000+ | High-volume production, multiple channels |

Underspending on creative is the most common budget mistake. Running $50K per month in ad spend with $1,000 per month in creative production means you are showing the same fatigued ads to an increasingly saturated audience. CPA rises, ROAS falls, and you blame the media buyer when the creative pipeline was the bottleneck.

## What to Ask Before Signing

1. What is included in the monthly retainer versus what costs extra?
2. How many revision rounds are included per concept?
3. What are the payment terms — net 15, net 30, or prepaid?
4. Is there a minimum commitment period? What is the cancellation policy?
5. Do I own all content produced, including raw footage?
6. Is strategy included, or is it an add-on?
7. What happens if I need more content than the retainer covers?

The best agency relationships start with transparent pricing and clear scope. If you cannot get a straight answer on what things cost before signing, that opacity will not improve after you are a client.
