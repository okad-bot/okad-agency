UGC Agency Pricing in 2026: What Brands Pay

UGC agency pricing breakdown: per-video rates ($100-$500+), monthly retainers ($2,500-$10,000), project packages, and hidden costs to watch for.

UGC ads6 min readby the OKAD team

UGC agency pricing ranges from $100 to $500+ per video, with monthly retainers from $2,500 to $10,000+. The spread is wide because agencies bundle different services — some hand you raw creator footage, others deliver fully edited, tested, and optimized ad creatives. This guide breaks down what you are actually paying for so you can compare quotes and budget accurately.

Per-Video Pricing ($100–$500+)

Per-video pricing is the most transparent model. You pay a fixed rate for each finished asset. Rates vary based on production complexity.

Video TypePrice RangeWhat’s Included
Talking head (simple)$100–$200Script, 1 creator, basic edit, 1 revision
Product demo$150–$300Script, creator + product shipping, editing with b-roll, 2 revisions
Multi-scene production$300–$500+Custom script, multiple scenes/locations, professional editing, captions, 2–3 revisions
Whitelisted video+$100–$300/moCreator account access for running paid ads

Per-video pricing makes sense when you are:

  • Testing a new agency before committing to a retainer
  • Running one-off or seasonal campaigns
  • Producing fewer than 10 videos per month
  • Validating a new product or market before scaling

The downside is cost. Agencies price individual videos higher to account for the overhead of onboarding and context-switching between clients. If you consistently need volume, you will overpay on a per-video basis.

Monthly Retainers ($2,500–$10,000+)

Retainers lock in capacity at a lower per-unit cost. Most agencies structure them in tiers.

TierMonthly CostVideos/MonthWhat’s Included
Starter$2,500–$4,00010–15Scripts, creator management, editing, 2 revisions per video
Growth$4,000–$7,00015–30Above + creative testing, hook variations, performance reporting
Scale$7,000–$10,000+30–50+Above + dedicated creative strategist, whitelisting, priority turnaround

Retainer clients typically save 20–40% compared to per-video pricing. The agency can plan resources around your account, which means faster turnaround and more consistent quality.

Retainers make sense when you:

  • Need 10+ videos per month on a regular schedule
  • Run ongoing paid social campaigns that require fresh creative
  • Want a dedicated team that understands your brand without re-briefing every project

Most retainers have a 3-month minimum commitment. Some agencies offer month-to-month at a higher rate.

Project-Based Pricing ($3,000–$10,000)

Project pricing works best for defined campaigns with a clear scope: a product launch, seasonal push, or market entry.

A typical project package includes:

  • Discovery and strategy — audience research, competitor creative audit, messaging angles
  • Batch production — 8–15 videos produced in a single production cycle
  • Variations for testing — hook swaps, CTA changes, different creators for the same script

For example, a launch package of 10 videos with 3 hook variations each gives you 30 testable assets — enough to run meaningful creative tests from day one. At OKAD, we structure launch packages this way because raw video count matters less than the number of distinct concepts you can test.

Project pricing usually falls between per-video and retainer rates on a per-unit basis, since the agency can batch production efficiently but is not guaranteed ongoing work.

What’s Included (and What’s Not)

Before comparing quotes, understand what is standard and what gets billed as an add-on. This varies significantly between agencies.

Typically included in the base price:

  • Scriptwriting and concept development
  • Creator sourcing and vetting
  • Production management and communication
  • Basic editing (cuts, captions, music)
  • 1–2 revision rounds

Often billed separately:

Add-OnTypical Cost
Rush delivery (48h or less)+25–50% surcharge
Additional revision rounds$50–$100 per round
Extended usage rights (12+ months)$100–$500 per video
Music/sound licensing (premium tracks)$50–$200 per video
Whitelisting management$100–$300/mo per creator
Creative strategy and reporting$500–$2,000/mo
Platform-specific reformatting$25–$75 per variation

Always ask for an itemized quote. An agency quoting $200/video with three add-ons can end up costing more than one quoting $350/video all-inclusive. A solid creative brief also reduces surprise costs by minimizing revision cycles.

Creator Rates vs. Agency Rates

Individual UGC creators charge $50–$500 per video depending on their experience, niche, and deliverables. On the surface, that looks cheaper than agency rates. The math changes when you factor in what the agency handles.

Agency markup covers:

  • Sourcing — finding and vetting creators from a roster (not cold outreach)
  • Project management — briefs, timelines, communication, feedback loops
  • Editing — professional post-production, not raw creator footage
  • Quality control — scripts reviewed before shooting, footage reviewed before delivery
  • Revisions — managed rounds with clear turnaround times

When you manage creators directly, you absorb all of that work. For 1–5 videos, it is manageable. Above 10 videos per month, the time cost of managing creators yourself — finding them, briefing them, chasing deliverables, giving feedback, re-editing — typically exceeds the agency premium.

The breakeven point for most brands is around 10 videos per month. Below that, direct creator relationships can save money. Above that, an agency usually delivers better cost-per-usable-asset because their revision rates are lower and their processes are built for volume.

How to Compare Agency Quotes

Not all quotes are created equal. Here is how to evaluate them side by side.

Compare cost per usable asset, not cost per video. If Agency A delivers 20 videos with a 30% revision rate and Agency B delivers 15 videos where 90% are usable on the first or second round, Agency B is the better deal. Entry-level or unmanaged creators have revision rates of 30–50%. Experienced agencies with strong briefing processes run at 10–15%.

Checklist for comparing quotes:

  • What is the per-video rate at your expected volume?
  • How many revision rounds are included?
  • What is the average turnaround time?
  • Are scripts, editing, and captions included or extra?
  • Is there a minimum commitment or contract length?
  • What usage rights are granted (duration, platforms, paid media)?
  • Is whitelisting included or billed separately?
  • Do they provide performance reporting or creative strategy?

Request sample timelines for your expected volume. An agency promising 30 videos per month but quoting a 3-week turnaround per batch may not actually deliver the velocity you need.

Recommended Budgets by Company Stage

Your content budget should scale with your ad spend and testing velocity. Here is a practical breakdown.

StageMonthly Content BudgetVideos/MonthRecommended Model

Growth$4,000–$7,00015–30Growth retainer
Scale ($50K+/mo ad spend)$7,000–$15,00030–60+Scale retainer + whitelisting

At the testing stage, focus on learning which concepts, creators, and formats perform. You do not need 50 videos — you need 10 good ones that teach you something.

At growth stage, you have winning concepts and need variations and fresh angles to prevent ad fatigue. This is where retainers pay for themselves through volume discounts and consistent output.

At scale, creative is your bottleneck. You need a production partner who can match your media buying velocity with enough variety to keep CPAs stable. Whitelisting becomes essential at this level since branded accounts fatigue faster than creator-fronted ads.

Choosing the Right Model

Match your pricing model to your volume and commitment level. If you are producing fewer than 10 videos per month, start with per-video or project pricing. Once you consistently need 10+ videos, a retainer will save money and improve quality through a dedicated team that knows your brand.

Questions people ask

How much do UGC agencies charge per video?

Most UGC agencies charge $100–$500+ per finished video depending on complexity, creator tier, and included services. Simple talking-head videos start around $100–$200. Multi-scene productions with custom scripts, props, and professional editing run $300–$500+. These rates typically include scripting, creator management, and basic editing.

Is a monthly retainer or per-video pricing better?

Monthly retainers save 20–40% compared to per-video pricing and guarantee capacity. They make sense when you need 10+ videos per month consistently. Per-video pricing works for testing or occasional campaigns where you do not need ongoing production.

What hidden costs should I watch for with UGC agencies?

Common hidden costs include: revision fees beyond 1-2 rounds, rush delivery surcharges (25-50%), extended usage rights or platform licensing, music/sound licensing, whitelisting access fees, and strategy or reporting as paid add-ons. Always ask for an itemized quote before signing.

How much should I budget for UGC testing?

Start with $2,000–$5,000 per month for content production (10–25 videos), plus your ad spend budget for testing. This gives you enough creative volume to test concepts and identify winners. Scaling brands invest $5,000–$15,000 monthly for 25–60+ videos.
