Jumpman Rebranding — Franchise-Ready Brand Identity & Web Design for Paris Fitness Studio

Jumpman is a trampoline fitness studio in Paris, founded in 2016. They needed a complete rebrand and a new website to support franchise expansion across France — a digital presence that would attract both fitness clients and potential franchise partners.

The Challenge

Jumpman had three problems holding back growth:

  • Outdated brand identity — the existing visual style couldn’t scale beyond a single location
  • No franchise-ready assets — potential partners had nothing professional to evaluate
  • Dual audience — the website needed to convert both B2C (class bookings) and B2B (franchise inquiries) without confusing either group

Brand Strategy & Positioning

Before any design work, we ran a full brand strategy sprint:

  • Competitive analysis of the Paris fitness market
  • Audience segmentation — individual clients vs. franchise partners
  • Brand positioning around Jumpman’s unique energy and community culture

This phase shaped every decision that followed.

Brand Identity — “Phantom Stage” Concept

We developed three brand directions:

  • Energy, Power in Motion — urban, graffiti-inspired
  • Neon Beats — high-energy nightlife aesthetics
  • Phantom Stage — theatrical, elegant, expressive

Jumpman chose Phantom Stage. The concept reframed fitness as performance — participants aren’t just working out, they’re on stage.

Their mascot — a bulldog — was reimagined as a dramatic “Phantom” character with a flowing cape, theatrical lighting, and dynamic gradients. A spotlight became the core graphic element across all brand touchpoints.

Website Design for Two Audiences

The website serves two distinct user journeys:

  • For clients (B2C): clear booking flow, class schedules, studio atmosphere — optimized for mobile
  • For franchise partners (B2B): business model overview, investment details, and a professional inquiry form

UI features smooth animations, intuitive navigation, and immersive visuals tied to the theatrical brand identity.

Franchise Toolkit & Brand Guidelines

To support scalable franchise rollout, we delivered:

  • Bilingual brand guidelines (English and Russian)
  • Ready-to-use social media and marketing templates
  • Franchise presentation deck for partner onboarding

Results

  • Brand recognition — Jumpman stands out in Paris’s competitive fitness market with a distinctive theatrical identity
  • Franchise interest — professional brand presentation tripled partner inquiries
  • Higher conversions — dual-purpose website increased both class bookings and franchise leads
  • Scalable system — brand guidelines and asset library enable consistent rollout to new locations

Frequently Asked Questions

How did the theater concept solve the dual-audience problem?

The theatrical framing — drama, shadows, Phantom-style aesthetics — communicated intensity for athletes while making the experience aspirational and Instagram-worthy for newcomers. It raised perceived experience value while lowering the intimidation barrier.

How long does a complete rebranding and web design process take?

Typically 2-4 months depending on scope. Jumpman's comprehensive transformation — brand strategy, identity, mascot redesign, website, and franchise toolkit — was achieved within 2-3 months.

Why design for franchise expansion before opening the first franchise?

Retrofitting brand consistency is expensive. Building a scalable system (guidelines, templates, signage specs, digital assets) from day one means every future location launches with brand consistency built in — without a designer making decisions at every step.
