High ROAS UGC Video Ads — Modular Systems for E-Commerce & Apps

ZenToes was preparing for their Walmart retail launch. Skribly needed Tier 1 English-speaking users for their drawing app. YourChristianDate had to reach a religious dating audience without sounding preachy. Three completely different products — and the same modular video ad system drove results for all of them.

ZenToes — 60+ Testable Variations for a Walmart Launch

When foot care brand ZenToes prepared to enter Walmart, they needed ad creative that could perform across Meta, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts simultaneously.

We produced:

  • 10 static ad concepts with multiple layout variations
  • 10 video concepts using 6 different script frameworks
  • 3-4 hook variations per video for immediate A/B testing
  • Platform-specific edits optimized for Meta feed, TikTok native, and YouTube Shorts

The math: 10 concepts x 3 hooks x 2 CTAs = 60+ testable variations. Each variation isolates a single variable, so every dollar spent on testing produces actionable data instead of guesswork.

Result: 8%+ view-to-25-second retention rate — significantly above the 5% industry benchmark for health and wellness products.

Skribly — 1,257 Videos, 1.5M Views, 2,000 Registrations

Skribly is a step-by-step drawing app (AppStore, iPad + Apple Pencil). Starting point: ~100 downloads per week. Goal: acquire Tier 1 English-speaking users.

Competitive research identified the winning format: faceless POV drawing content. Just a hand, a pencil, a voiceover, and a story. The best-performing variation was multiplayer drawing — two people drawing together, reacting to each other, with something unexpected happening.

  • 2 creators, 4 accounts, 3 platforms
  • Month 1: 90 videos
  • Month 2: 195 videos
  • Total: 1,257 videos → 1.5M views → 2,000 registrations

One critical decision changed everything: manual posting from a US-based team member. TikTok’s algorithm is highly sensitive to geo-signals — posting from outside the target market tanks distribution.

YourChristianDate — From Scripts to 1M+ Organic Views

YourChristianDate (Dating.com Group) targets a religious dating audience. Constraint: no paid traffic for organic content. Goal: 60 videos/month, 1,000-2,000 views per video, +15% each month.

The audience values their faith deeply but does not want to be preached at. Content gets judged emotionally first, ideologically second.

What worked: Observational, first-timer humor. “Dating at 45 is weird and here’s why that’s funny.” Two creator archetypes resonated: a younger attractive male and a confident man around 50 — both US-based.

What bombed: Preachy and authority-based formats. Anything positioning the platform as a moral authority failed completely.

Result: individual videos hit millions of organic views. The organic engine became the foundation for paid campaigns that outperformed studio-produced creative.

VueSwiss — ~30 Videos Per Day at Scale

VueSwiss (Swiss skincare — nose patches, toners, skin care) became our template for high-volume production:

  • 10+ base videos per day, each with 3+ hook variations
  • ~30 videos total per day
  • Stable orders running 2-3 months straight

VueSwiss proved that when you systematize hook variation, creator rotation, and editing workflows, you can sustain enormous daily output without creative fatigue.

Upvote — Full Performance Cycle, CPA ~EUR 65

Upvote is a marketing platform for creators. We ran the complete performance cycle: PCA research, strategy, scripts, creator sourcing, editing, launch, analysis.

  • UGC scripts with hooks tailored for podcasters, marketers, and creators
  • Each script: personal hook → problem → solution → CTA
  • 11 video creatives, CPA ~EUR 65, CTR 6-12%

Why Modular Beats Linear

Traditional production: write a script, film it, edit it, launch it. If it underperforms, you have one data point and zero alternatives.

Our modular approach decomposes every video into independent components (hook, body, CTA), produces variations of each, and tests systematically:

  • Hook testing data across 200+ campaigns shows hooks account for 60-70% of overall ad performance variance
  • A mediocre body with a great hook outperforms a great body with a mediocre hook every time

Cross-Platform Optimization

Each platform rewards different content behaviors. We produce platform-specific edits from the same raw footage:

  • Meta: Captions essential (85% sound-off), slower pacing, stronger visual hooks
  • TikTok: Native feel, faster cuts, trending audio, creator-forward framing
  • YouTube Shorts: SEO-optimized titles, slightly longer hooks, stronger CTAs

Frequently Asked Questions

How many video variations do you produce per campaign?

It depends on the product and platform. ZenToes: 10 concepts expanded into 60+ testable variations. Skribly: 1,257 videos across 4 accounts. VueSwiss: ~30 videos per day. The system scales to whatever volume your testing budget supports.

What retention rates should I expect from UGC video ads?

Industry average is 3-5% view-to-25-second retention. Our modular system delivered 8%+ for ZenToes by isolating and optimizing hooks independently from body content.

Can this system work for non-physical products like apps or dating platforms?

Yes. Skribly (iPad drawing app) and YourChristianDate (dating platform) both used the same modular framework as physical product campaigns. The creative direction changes, but the production structure stays the same.
