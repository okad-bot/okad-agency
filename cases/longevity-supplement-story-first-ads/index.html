68 Story-First Ads. Product Waits 21 Seconds.

68 story-first ads. Product waits 21 seconds. A European brand launched a daily longevity drink for people 40 and older. They had a launch film, a nutrition scientist on camera and a stack of creator clips. I turned that footage into a library of ads built on three things: a scientist who explains, real people with real stories, and AI b-roll and charts that show what happens inside the body. The product never shows up before the story has done its job.

What I delivered

  • 68 finished ads in the English library
  • 27 scripts, each cut in up to 4 hook variations
  • 6 story formats tested (confession, mom story, testimonial, ingredient story, kinetic manifesto, scientist explains)
  • 2 languages — English and German
  • AI b-roll: glowing cells, molecules, organs, and data charts made for each script
  • Scientist cut every 2.5 seconds with visuals under every sentence

The winning pattern: story-first structure where the product enters only after the viewer is emotionally invested — 21 seconds in for the best-performing “Robert” ad.
