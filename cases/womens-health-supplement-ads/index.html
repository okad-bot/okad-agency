Q6 · Can you even advertise this on Meta?

Yes, as long as no line promises a cure.

Health is one of the most restricted ad categories. Safety was built into the scripts from the first draft, not fixed in moderation.

Rule 1

No cure language

Stories describe how someone feels, never what the product treats.

Rule 2

Said out loud

"It doesn't replace your doctor. It's not a treatment." Spoken in the video, not hidden in the caption.

Rule 3

Doctor in the loop

Any change to medication happens "under her supervision", never on the viewer's own.

approval

One round of comments

The client got a short doc: batch structure, angle matrix, full hooks, bodies as beats and in full. Their edits were minor wording, made in the same doc with comments kept.

Q7 · How do you make four hooks a fair test?

We edit in code, so only the hook changes.

The edit is written frame by frame, not dragged on a timeline. The main part is rendered once, and each variant is the hook joined to it without re-encoding. Four files, one identical body, and a fix in the body is one rebuild instead of four.

See how the edit was engineered

Pauses cut by speech, not by eye

Gaps over 0.45 s shrink to 0.22 s: still a breath, not a hole. A map of original time to edit time drives every B-roll, caption and graphic.

Lip-sync in three steps

Word-level alignment at 10 ms, matching words in order so short words don't latch onto the wrong spot, then a smooth time-warp with a knot every ~2 s so motion never jitters.

A hidden offset, found and fixed

In every talking-head clip the picture started 0.2–0.8 s after the sound. Measured clip by clip; the missing frames went to the previous B-roll shot.

Two shots no warp could save

Replaced with pop-up cards on a clean background that land exactly on their word, so the meaning reads with sound off.

Mastering without delay

The standard limiter adds 5 ms of lookahead and shifts the track. Peaks are tamed by a gain curve instead, and checked again after AAC encoding.

Picture never re-encoded

Every sound fix is a remux with the video copied. Delivery: 1.4 GB in 74 parts, rebuilt on the client side, 8 of 8 matched by checksum.
