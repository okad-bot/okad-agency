Q1 · What is canvas UGC?

The app screen is the creator.

Hands and an Apple Pencil only. A hook on screen, a small story while the drawing grows, a reveal at the end. The viewer watches the product work without being sold to. Every creator tested at least five new formats a week; these four held up.

Multiplayer

Two people, one canvas

They draw together and write to each other. The strongest format.

Q2 · What worked best?

Drawing together, talking on the canvas.

The best variation was multiplayer drawing. Two people share one canvas, react to each other and write messages instead of speaking. Something unexpected always happens. That tension and its resolution keep people watching until the picture is done.

"I'm fire." "You are water."WRITTEN ON THE CANVAS

Became a little rivalry. Red and blue fight over the shape, then the finished owls hug. The ending pays off the conflict from the first second.

"What is it?" "Why you drawing so long?"ONE PLAYER TEASES THE OTHER

Became a question people stay for. The viewer wants to know what the picture is, the same way the second player does.

"I can't, I'm famous." "Let's be friends?"A CONVERSATION IN HANDWRITING

Became a story about strangers. Shared canvases feel social, which is exactly what makes people comment and ask what app this is.

Q3 · Did I test a face too?

I did. The canvas won.

One account ran classic talking-head UGC: a creator reacting to camera, "I found THE BEST drawing app!!!" The same afternoon I screenshotted both profiles. Faceless canvas videos were sitting at several thousand views each. The face videos mostly stayed in the hundreds.

For marketersHype reviews read as ads in a creative niche. Showing the drawing happen is proof. Telling people it's the best app is a claim.

Q4 · How do you post 1,257 videos?

Creators on a schedule, KPIs that climb weekly.

Every creator signed the same terms: three videos a day, 70 to 90 a month, Tier 1 country only. Pay was tied to average views, with a bonus when creators came up with working ideas on their own. Month one shipped 90 videos. Month two shipped 195.

For marketersTikTok reads location signals hard. All posting was done by hand from a team member in the US, so every account looked local to the market I wanted.
