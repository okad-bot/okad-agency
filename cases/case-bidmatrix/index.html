Bit Matrix — Brand Identity & Wix Website for an AdTech Platform

Bit Matrix is a B2B technology platform managing advertising integrations within mobile applications. They came to us with a clear challenge: “We like what our competitors are doing, but we don’t want to look like them.”

This is common among AdTech brands — founders admire competitor aesthetics but know differentiation is the key to growth, especially when pitching new clients and partners.

Project Scope

Our collaboration with Bit Matrix included:

  • Full brand development — visual identity and tone of voice
  • Sales-ready media deck for partnerships
  • Dark-theme responsive Wix website
  • Social media templates for channel consistency

Brand Direction & Custom Visual Elements

The initial direction was influenced by a competitor’s visual style. Our job was to take that energy and turn it into something entirely original.

  • We tested various layout systems and typographic structures
  • We explored graphic forms suggesting tech, scale, and control
  • We built a proprietary set of graphic elements, fully unique to Bit Matrix

The final design language positioned Bit Matrix as confident, modern, and distinctly not another cookie-cutter AdTech brand.

Color & Contrast Strategy

  • Dark foundation with three strong accent colors
  • Visual impact with enhanced accessibility and modularity
  • Scalable system — easy to apply across website, media materials, and social content

The Website

To launch quickly without sacrificing quality, we built the site on Wix with a custom design system.

Key UX/UI decisions:

  • Fixed grid-based structure that works across desktop and mobile
  • Dark UI with high-contrast highlights for a sleek, tech-forward feel
  • Modular design elements reflecting product logic and hierarchy

The result is a professional, lightweight, and responsive site delivering clarity, confidence, and conversion — all without custom code.

The Media Deck

While the site focused on boldness and energy, the media deck needed a calmer, more readable format for sales conversations.

  • Light backgrounds and simplified typography
  • Layout guiding readers through positioning, features, and benefits
  • Visual elements mirroring the website style for brand consistency

Together, the site and media deck created a unified brand ecosystem — one that works in both public and private channels.

Results

  • A cohesive, scalable brand identity for digital and offline use
  • A high-converting website that clearly communicates value
  • A sales-ready media deck simplifying the pitch process
  • A design system differentiating Bit Matrix from lookalike competitors
  • Positive client feedback and referrals to other tech companies

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a no-code Wix website look premium for a B2B tech brand?

Yes. With a custom design system, grid-based layout, and dark-mode UI, Bit Matrix's Wix site looks and performs like a custom-built product — launched faster and easier to maintain.

How do you differentiate an AdTech brand from competitors?

We build proprietary visual elements instead of using industry-standard templates. For Bit Matrix, this meant custom graphic forms and a unique three-accent color system on a dark foundation.

How long does a brand identity + website project take?

Bit Matrix's full scope — brand identity, responsive website, media deck, and social templates — was delivered in 3-5 weeks from kickoff.
