5Public — Brand Unification & Templates for a 50-Outlet Media Network

5Public is a rapidly growing network of 50 regional media outlets across Russia. Each outlet operated independently with its own logo, visual language, and content templates. As the network scaled, this fragmentation became a bottleneck.

The client had three goals:

  • Build a unified visual identity across all 50 publications
  • Make it scalable for non-designers and professionals alike
  • Preserve the local identity of each city-based channel

They had tried solving this internally, but without shared design standards, alignment was impossible.

Strategy & Concept Development

We started with research across the entire 5Public ecosystem:

  • Audit of all existing visual assets from different regions
  • Competitive analysis of media brands with a national-local split
  • Stakeholder interviews to understand team workflows and platform limitations

From this emerged a clear vision: a modular brand system where each local outlet retains its uniqueness but fits into a broader, instantly recognizable identity.

The Core Branding Solution

The main breakthrough was the logo system. Rather than replacing each outlet’s local character, we amplified it using map-based identifiers — each city’s outline became part of its official logo.

We paired this with:

  • A consistent font and layout system
  • A color-coding scheme for regional identity organization
  • Simplified logo variants and watermark versions for cross-platform use

Guidelines & Templates for Scale

Design systems are only as good as their documentation. We built an ecosystem of guides and toolkits for daily use:

  • Full visual style guide — typography, colors, logo rules, and layout ratios
  • Social media templates (15+ categories) — daily posts, news, carousels, and event coverage
  • Low-quality imagery instructions — a frequent challenge in fast-moving local news
  • Cross-platform assets — optimized for desktop and mobile, including Telegram and VK formats

We focused on enabling non-designers — local editors, marketers, and journalists — to work quickly and confidently inside a unified visual language.

Infrastructure for Growth

A crucial part of brand unification is backend clarity:

  • 50 individual logo kits organized by city
  • Google Drive asset library categorized by region and format
  • Flexible templates allowing rapid adaptation with or without a designer
  • Media kit template for sponsors and partners

This system reduced bottlenecks, cut duplicate requests, and enabled instant onboarding of new regions or staff.

Results

  • A unified yet flexible brand identity for 50 media channels
  • Consistent look-and-feel across VK, Telegram, and internal documents
  • Templates used daily by both professional designers and local content teams
  • Full rebrand rolled out across all cities in under 2 months
  • Streamlined external communications with a ready-to-use media kit

The client now has a scalable visual system that supports daily content creation, partnership outreach, and continued network expansion.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you unify branding across 50+ outlets without losing local identity?

We use modular visual elements — in 5Public's case, city map outlines integrated into each local logo — so every outlet is recognizable as part of the network while keeping its own character.

Can non-designers use brand templates effectively?

Yes. We built all 15+ template categories for use in Figma and Canva with step-by-step documentation, so local editors and journalists can produce on-brand content without design skills.

How long does a full rebrand for a media network take?

We completed 5Public's rebrand in under 2 months — including 50 unique logos, a media kit, social templates, and full brand guidelines.
