Jumpman — Franchise-Ready Website Design for Paris Fitness Studio

Jumpman is the first trampoline fitness studio in Paris, operating since 2016. With new locations opening and franchise interest growing, the brand needed a website that could convert both everyday fitness clients (B2C) and potential franchise partners (B2B).

Strategic Website Goals

We defined four non-negotiables with the Jumpman team:

  • Frictionless booking in 2 clicks — booking a class had to feel as easy as jumping
  • High-impact atmosphere — visitors needed to feel the drama, lights, and movement from the homepage
  • Guidance for newcomers — a dedicated “First Time?” page addressing unspoken fears
  • Franchise-ready infrastructure — strategic copywriting and visual storytelling that speaks to investors

UX & Web Design Features

The end-to-end web experience combined UX strategy, visual storytelling, and conversion optimization:

  • Dual-purpose navigation — optimized paths for B2C first-timers and B2B researchers
  • Custom UI with theatrical transitions — layered visuals supporting the “Phantom Stage” brand identity
  • Responsive animations — movement and energy reflected across all devices
  • Integrated session videos — lights, jumps, shadows, and sweat captured in embedded footage
  • Modular components — designed for faster, cheaper future franchise site launches

Conversion-Focused Design

Every design choice prioritized performance alongside aesthetics:

  • Clear CTAs always within reach on every page
  • Minimalistic forms with auto-validation to reduce friction
  • Mobile-first booking experience for on-the-go users
  • Fast-loading visuals despite complex animations

Results

  • Doubled B2C bookings within the first month of launch
  • 54% increase in session time — users spent more time exploring videos and testimonials
  • 41% reduction in drop-off rate thanks to smoother funnel and visual storytelling
  • Franchise materials integrated seamlessly into the site, boosting credibility and streamlining partner onboarding

Frequently Asked Questions

How did the website serve both fitness clients and franchise partners?

Navigation was optimized for two journeys: B2C visitors land on class schedules and 2-click booking, while B2B partners access franchise materials, brand positioning, and investment details — all without cluttering either experience.

What made the booking flow convert so well?

We reduced booking to 2 clicks with auto-validated forms, mobile-first layout, and CTAs always within reach. Combined with immersive video content, session time increased by 54% and drop-off fell by 41%.

How long did the website project take?

The full custom website — including UX strategy, UI design, development, copywriting, and franchise integration — was delivered in 6-10 weeks.
