In a Woman — Brand Identity for a Women-in-Tech Accelerator

In a Woman is an international accelerator focused on democratizing access to entrepreneurship through NoCode tools. The program offers NoCode education for women founders, project-based learning, hackathons, grants, and global community support. As their presence grew across markets, they needed a brand system that matched their ambition.

The Challenge

The accelerator was rapidly scaling but lacked a cohesive visual identity. Their online presence, event branding, and printed materials did not yet reflect the strength of their mission.

Key issues:

  • Inconsistent visuals across platforms
  • No scalable template system for content production
  • Lack of recognizable brand elements for merchandise and events
  • Need for inclusive, bold, and tech-forward design language

Brand Identity and Visual Language

We built a flexible, modern brand system centered around:

  • Color palette: evokes energy, safety, and professionalism without tech-world cliches
  • Typography: balances clarity and boldness
  • Logo system: works across digital and physical applications — from pitch decks to event signage

The design honored the women in the program not as learners, but as future founders and leaders.

Template System for Digital and Events

To ensure scalability, we delivered:

  • Social media templates: announcements, speaker intros, testimonials
  • Event signage: roll-up banners for hackathons and summits
  • Branded decks and worksheets: for workshops and mentor sessions

The visual system was built to be used and reused by a small team without design skills.

Merchandise and Print Collateral

To bring the brand into the physical world, we created:

  • Apparel: T-shirts, tote bags, and caps with smart typographic design
  • Accessories: stickers for participants and mentors
  • Print assets: brochures and event schedules

The result was a brand experience that felt tactile, intentional, and community-driven.

Results

  • Unified visual identity across all platforms and events
  • Scalable templates that empower the team to produce content independently
  • Branded merchandise that boosts community engagement and visibility
  • International-ready brand system for growth and partnership building

The branding system is now used by the In a Woman team for every touchpoint — from applications to hackathons, from Lisbon to international markets.
