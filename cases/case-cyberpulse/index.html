Cyber Pulse — Content-Driven Sales for an Automotive Brand in Portugal

Cyber Pulse is an automotive content and community brand built for the Russian-speaking audience in Portugal. They needed a content machine that generates trust, traffic, and ultimately leads — without relying on cold calls or traditional advertising.

The Challenge

Unlike mass-market car dealerships or auto blogs, Cyber Pulse had to solve several problems at once:

  • Educate a niche audience about complex car import logistics
  • Build trust in a community-first format
  • Convert content views into car requests and purchases
  • Operate bilingually — Russian and English — for different audience segments

Content Planning and Editorial Strategy

We developed a detailed content plan based on:

  • Local insights from our Lisbon-based team
  • Seasonal trends in auto imports
  • Audience pain points — taxes, insurance, legal processes

We sourced a copywriter with deep automotive knowledge, designed all visuals in line with the brand book, and tailored every post to platform behavior — what works in Telegram does not always work on Instagram.

Team and Workflow

We assembled a hybrid team:

  • Content Manager (Lisbon-based): strategic oversight and planning
  • Automotive Copywriter: expert writing and research
  • Designer: car visuals, infographics, Instagram assets
  • Collaboration tools: Telegram + Notion for streamlined workflow
  • Strategy calls: regular client reviews to track performance and adjust direction

Multiformat, Platform-Native Content

We published content daily across multiple channels:

  • Telegram Channel: articles, tips, updates, client stories
  • Telegram Chat: a live, engaged community of enthusiasts and leads
  • Instagram: storytelling, car visuals, highlights in English and Russian
  • Infographics and Case Studies: simplifying complex topics visually

Paid Distribution with Real-Time Optimization

We managed advertising placements in 10-30 Telegram channels per month:

  • Negotiating rates with channel owners
  • Tracking cost-per-click, cost-per-lead, and cost-per-sale
  • Weekly reporting and campaign optimization

We also ran Meta Ads targeting expats and Russian-speaking users in Portugal.

Content Funnel Design

We designed a content funnel that moved users from attention to conversion:

  • Top of funnel: engaging car stories, community buzz, useful tips
  • Middle of funnel: real client cases, testimonials, before/after
  • Bottom of funnel: Telegram chat access, price breakdowns, car import timelines
  • Automation: lead qualification through Telegram bots and email flows

Results After 12 Months

  • 1,500+ subscribers in both Telegram channel and chat (from zero)
  • 36,000+ Instagram followers with consistently high engagement
  • Significant lead growth directly from content
  • Optimized ad spend through tracking and iterative testing
  • Automated lead capture through bots and flows
  • Zero cold calls throughout the entire period

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Telegram be used as a sales channel for a niche business?

Yes. For Cyber Pulse, Telegram served as a full sales funnel — from content and community chat to lead qualification via bots — generating consistent leads without cold calls.

How long does it take to build a content-driven audience from zero?

Cyber Pulse grew from 0 to 1,500+ Telegram subscribers and 36,000+ Instagram followers in 12 months with daily bilingual content and paid placements.

Do you manage content in multiple languages?

Yes. This project included both Russian and English content, with tone, messaging, and visuals tailored to each audience segment.
