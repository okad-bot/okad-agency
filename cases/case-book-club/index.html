Book Club — Content Strategy & Social Media Design for a Reading Community

Anna is the founder of a book club community focused on nonfiction, psychology, and business literature. Her model includes 9-12 active groups of ~10 participants each, facilitated by experts in sociology, psychology, and entrepreneurship. The club had strong retention and word-of-mouth growth, but the lack of consistent content was slowing scale and draining her energy.

She made a crucial decision: delegate content marketing while preserving her voice.

The Founder’s Fear of Letting Go

Most personal brand creators hesitate before delegating content. Common fears include:

  • “No one will capture my tone.”
  • “What if the content feels fake to my audience?”
  • “I’ll lose control of the brand.”
  • “How do I balance authenticity and sales?”

These concerns are valid — but they’re also what keeps founders from scaling.

Our Strategic Approach

Audience-First Research

We began by mapping Anna’s audience motivations and triggers:

  • Why they joined the club
  • What content converted them
  • What objections they had to purchasing
  • How they described value in their own words

This shaped both the message and the funnel.

A Personalized Content Funnel

We created a modular, step-by-step funnel moving users from awareness to action:

  • Storytelling content for engagement
  • Value-focused education content
  • Direct but warm product prompts
  • Seasonal campaigns with urgency elements

Every piece of content had a role. No more random posts.

UX-Led Design for Social Media

Complex ideas (like how the club works or who leads it) were turned into scroll-stopping carousel posts using:

  • Clean typography
  • Content structuring techniques
  • Visual storytelling logic

We made the product easy to understand at a glance.

Expanding the Brand’s Voice

We introduced a new content track — fiction and literature — to attract a broader audience while staying aligned with the brand. This created new conversion opportunities and gave the brand more storytelling tools.

Consistent, High-Quality Output

We delivered:

  • 23 custom-designed carousel posts in 3 months
  • Reusable content templates
  • Merch campaigns (a paper book giveaway with a 15 EUR budget reached 22k+ accounts)
  • Community-building tools

All without requiring Anna’s daily involvement.

Results After 3 Months

Content Performance:

  • +1,134% reach growth (155,000+ accounts)
  • 23 high-performing posts
  • 594 new subscribers
  • 7,684 profile visits

Sales and Funnel Metrics:

  • 8% conversion from profile views
  • Groups filled 2x faster
  • Signups closed 2 weeks before the month started
  • Monthly launch rhythm established

Business Impact:

  • 20+ new clients/month
  • Avg subscription: 7,000-10,000 RUB/month
  • Annual LTV per client: 144,000 RUB
  • Projected annual revenue: 34,560,000 RUB

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you preserve a founder's authentic voice when delegating content?

We start with interviews and a content audit, then build a tone guide. Everything is written and designed to feel like the founder on their best day — audiences can't tell the difference.

What results can content marketing deliver for a small community business?

For this book club, we achieved +1,134% reach growth, 594 new subscribers, and groups filling 2x faster — all within 3 months on a modest budget.

How many posts per month do you need to grow a personal brand?

Quality over quantity. We delivered 23 custom carousel posts in 3 months (roughly 2 per week), each with a clear role in the content funnel — awareness, education, or conversion.
