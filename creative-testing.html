How it works

Most teams test 5-10 video ads per month. That's not enough data to find patterns. Here's what we do differently.

How most brands do it
  • Hire a videographer or agency for 5-10 videos
  • Pick the "best" creative based on gut feeling
  • Run it until it burns out
  • Start from scratch next month
  • No data on what actually worked or why
Our approach
  • 40-70 videos per batch, each testing one idea
  • Modular scripts: 3 hooks × 2 creators × 2 formats = 12 videos from one concept
  • Real purchase data after 2-3 weeks
  • Scale winners, kill losers, iterate the middle
  • You know exactly what worked and why
Step 1

Research & hypotheses

We analyze your competitors' ads, your existing data, customer reviews. We build 5-8 testing angles — real pain points your customers have.

Step 2

Scripts & creators

Modular scripts: one proven body, multiple openings. We match 2-3 creators from our 600+ network to each concept based on product fit.

Step 3

Production & launch

Creators shoot, we edit, first batch goes live as ads. 40-70 videos in 2 weeks. UGC, voiceover + B-roll, AI statics, product demos.

After 2-3 weeks of ad data, we tag every creative: Scale, Iterate, or Kill. Not likes — actual purchases. Then we scale winners and test new ideas.

What's inside each batch

Everything you need to go from zero to tested at scale.

Angles

5-8 reasons people buy your product

Real pain points, not marketing guesses. For supplements: bloating, fatigue, sleep. For AI products: speed, social proof, before/after. Each angle is a testing track.

Scripts

Modular: hook + body + CTA

3+ openings per concept. Written from research — competitor ads, customer surveys, your ad data. Scripts are the core of what we deliver.

Creators

The person matters more than the script

Same script can cost $32/customer with one creator and $92 with another. We test 2-3 creators per batch from our 600+ network to find the best fit.

Formats

6 formats, tested — not assumed

UGC talking head, voiceover + B-roll, before/after, product demo, POV, AI statics. In our data, VO + B-roll costs 25% less per customer than talking heads.

AI visuals

AI-generated scenes and statics at half the cost

AI statics brought customers at half the cost of traditional statics. Combined with UGC and scripts — AI handles visual variety while creators handle trust.
