AI UGC tool or AI UGC agency?

Tools like MakeUGC, Creatify or Arcads are great if you have time and someone who can write hooks and edit. You pay a subscription and do the work.

An agency makes sense when you don't want to learn prompts and editing, need the ads to look native, and want someone to read the results. I use these tools myself, along with image and video models, and my job is to make the output pass as a real post.

FAQ

What is AI UGC?

Video ads in the style of user-generated content, made fully or partly with AI: avatars, generated scenes, AI voice or AI product shots.

Do AI UGC ads work?

For testing angles and hooks, yes: you get more versions for the same budget. For testimonials and claims, real creators still build more trust.

Will Meta flag AI ads?

Meta asks to label some AI content. I follow platform rules and avoid fake testimonials.

Can you mix AI with my existing footage?

Yes, that's often the cheapest route. See modular production.
