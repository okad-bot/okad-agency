Our Process

Every animation project follows a structured four-step workflow that keeps timelines tight and revisions minimal. Most projects go from kickoff to final delivery in five to seven business days.

Step 01

Creative Brief & Storyboard

We start with a detailed creative brief to understand your goals, audience, brand guidelines, and where the animation will be used. From there, we develop a storyboard that maps out the visual narrative frame by frame. You see exactly what the final animation will look like before any motion work begins. This step eliminates surprises and ensures alignment on creative direction, messaging, and pacing before we invest production hours. We also define the technical specifications: aspect ratios, durations, file formats, and any platform-specific requirements.

Step 02

Style Frames & Mood

Once the storyboard is approved, we produce high-fidelity style frames that define the visual identity of the animation. These are fully rendered static frames that establish the color palette, typography treatment, illustration style, iconography, and overall art direction. You get two to three style directions to choose from or refine. This step is where the look and feel of your animation is locked in. It is far more efficient to iterate on style at this stage than to rework finished animation, so we invest the time here to get it right.

Step 03

Animation Production

With storyboard and style frames approved, our motion designers bring everything to life. We animate each scene with attention to timing, easing curves, transitions, and rhythm. Sound design and music are layered in during this phase to create a cohesive viewing experience. For explainer videos, we sync animation to voiceover with precise lip-timing and emphasis cues. You receive a work-in-progress review at the midpoint so we can catch any adjustments early. Production typically takes three to four days depending on complexity and total duration.

Step 04

Review & Delivery

We deliver the completed animation for your review with a structured feedback process. Each project includes revision rounds to fine-tune timing, color, copy, or transitions. Once approved, we export final files in every format you need: MP4 for web and social, MOV with alpha channel for compositing, GIF for email and lightweight placements, and Lottie or JSON for interactive web and app implementations. All project files are organized, labeled, and delivered via cloud drive with a summary document that maps each asset to its intended use.

Where Motion Works

Motion design is not a nice-to-have. It is a performance tool. Here are the contexts where animation delivers the most measurable impact for brands.

Paid Advertising

Animated ads consistently outperform static creatives on click-through rate and cost per acquisition across Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and programmatic display. Motion captures attention in the first second, which is the moment that determines whether someone engages or scrolls past. We produce ad animations in multiple hook variations and aspect ratios so you can test systematically and scale the winners without producing entirely new assets each time.

Social Media Content

Algorithms on every major platform prioritize video and motion content over static images. Animated posts earn higher engagement rates, longer view times, and more shares. We produce motion content designed for the specific behaviors of each platform: fast-paced loops for TikTok, polished animations for LinkedIn, eye-catching Stories for Instagram, and autoplay-optimized clips for Facebook Feed. A consistent stream of animated content keeps your brand visible and your audience engaged.

Product Marketing

Landing pages with animated explainers convert at significantly higher rates than those with text or static images alone. Product feature animations reduce support tickets by showing users exactly how things work. App store preview videos drive more installs when they demonstrate the interface with smooth, professional animation rather than raw screen recordings. Whether you are launching a new product or repositioning an existing one, motion design makes your value proposition immediately tangible and memorable.

Brand Identity

A static logo and color palette are table stakes. Modern brand systems include animated logo reveals, motion guidelines, transition libraries, and kinetic typography treatments. These assets ensure your brand feels alive and consistent across every digital touchpoint: website headers, video intros and outros, email signatures, event screens, and app loading states. We build motion into your brand identity system so every piece of content you produce carries a cohesive visual signature.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical turnaround time for a motion design project?
Most projects are completed in five to seven business days from brief approval to final delivery. Simple animations like logo reveals or social media loops can be delivered in three to four days. Longer explainer videos or complex campaigns may take up to ten business days. We confirm the timeline during the creative brief stage so you know exactly when to expect deliverables. Rush delivery is available for time-sensitive launches.
What file formats do you deliver?
We deliver in every format you need for your intended use. Standard deliverables include MP4 (H.264 and H.265) for web and social media, MOV with alpha channel transparency for compositing over other footage or backgrounds, GIF for email and lightweight web placements, and Lottie or JSON files for interactive implementations in web and mobile apps. We also provide platform-specific exports optimized for Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn specifications. All files are organized and labeled by format, resolution, and intended placement.
Do you provide music and sound design?
Yes. Sound design is included in every animation project. We add sound effects, ambient audio, and transitions that enhance the visual experience and create a more immersive result. For background music, we source from licensed libraries and include the licensing cost in our pricing. If you have specific music requirements, a branded audio identity, or need original composition, we can accommodate that as well. Voiceover production, including script refinement, casting, and recording direction, is available as an add-on for explainer videos and narrated content.
How many revisions are included?
Every project includes two rounds of revisions as standard. A revision round covers changes to timing, color, typography, transitions, copy, and sound. Because we align closely during the storyboard and style frame stages, most projects require only minor adjustments at the review stage. Our first-delivery approval rate is above 85 percent. If your project requires more extensive iteration, we are happy to discuss additional revision rounds as part of the project scope.
What does motion design typically cost?
Pricing depends on the type and complexity of the animation. Simple logo animations and social media loops start at $500 to $1,500. Animated ad creatives with multiple variations typically range from $1,500 to $4,000. Full explainer videos with script, voiceover, and custom illustration range from $3,000 to $8,000. We are based in Portugal, which means you get European creative quality at rates that are 40 to 60 percent lower than comparable studios in the US or UK. Every project includes a detailed quote before work begins so there are no surprises.
Do you work in 2D, 3D, or both?
We specialize in 2D motion design, which covers the vast majority of brand, advertising, and social media animation needs. This includes flat and isometric illustration, kinetic typography, icon animation, character animation, infographic motion, and UI animation. For projects that require 3D elements such as product renders, architectural visualization, or photorealistic environments, we collaborate with specialized 3D artists within our network and manage the full production pipeline so you have a single point of contact throughout.
Do I receive the source project files?
Source files, including After Effects projects, Illustrator assets, and animation rigs, are available as an add-on. Our standard deliverables include all final rendered files in the formats you need for deployment. If you have an in-house team that needs to make future edits or build on the animation, we provide a source file package with organized layers, labeled compositions, and documentation. This is especially useful for clients who want to maintain and extend their motion assets internally over time.
