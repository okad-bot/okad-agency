Our Process

We have refined this workflow across hundreds of landing pages. Every step is designed to move fast and produce pages that perform from day one. Most projects go from kickoff to live page in two to three weeks.

Step 01

Research & Strategy

We start by understanding your business, your audience, and your traffic sources. We audit your current pages, analyze competitor landing experiences, and review your ad creative to ensure message match between the click and the page. We map out the visitor journey, identify the primary conversion action, and define the page structure based on what your audience needs to see before they convert. By the end of this step, you have a strategic brief that outlines page goals, target audience segments, messaging hierarchy, and success metrics.

Step 02

Wireframing

Before any visual design begins, we build low-fidelity wireframes that establish the information architecture and content flow of your page. This is where we decide what goes above the fold, how sections stack on mobile, where social proof appears, and how the CTA repeats through the scroll. You review the wireframe and approve the structure before we invest time in visual polish. This step prevents expensive redesigns later and ensures every pixel of the final page serves a conversion purpose.

Step 03

Visual Design

We translate the approved wireframe into a high-fidelity design in Figma that matches your brand system. Typography, color, imagery, iconography, and spacing are all dialed in for both desktop and mobile. We design with real copy, never lorem ipsum, because placeholder text hides layout problems that only surface after development. You receive a complete Figma prototype with interactive states, hover effects, and responsive breakpoints so you can see exactly how the page will look and feel before a single line of code is written.

Step 04

Development (Webflow/Custom)

We build your approved design in Webflow or custom HTML/CSS/JS depending on your tech stack and requirements. Webflow builds give you a visual editor so your team can make copy changes without a developer. Custom builds integrate directly into your existing codebase, CMS, or marketing stack. Every page is built with semantic HTML, optimized asset loading, and performance budgets that target sub-two-second load times. We connect forms, analytics, tracking pixels, and any third-party integrations your workflow requires.

Step 05

Launch & Optimization

We do not hand off a page and disappear. Before launch, we run a quality checklist covering cross-browser testing, mobile responsiveness, load speed, form submissions, analytics firing, and tracking pixel verification. After launch, we monitor initial performance data and provide recommendations for your first round of A/B tests. Clients who commit to ongoing optimization cycles typically see conversion rate improvements of 30 to 60 percent within the first 90 days as we systematically test headlines, layouts, CTAs, and social proof placement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to design and launch a landing page?
Our standard timeline is two to three weeks from kickoff to launch. This includes research, wireframing, visual design, development, and QA testing. The first week covers strategy, wireframes, and design. The second and third weeks cover development, revisions, and launch preparation. If you have an urgent campaign deadline, we offer expedited timelines for an additional fee. Rush projects can be delivered in as little as seven business days depending on page complexity and content readiness.
Should I choose Webflow or a custom-coded page?
It depends on your team and tech stack. Webflow is ideal if your marketing team needs to make copy and image changes without involving a developer, if you want a visual CMS, or if you do not have an existing codebase to integrate with. Custom code is the better choice if your page needs to live inside an existing application, if you require complex integrations with your backend, or if you have strict performance requirements. We will recommend the right approach during the strategy phase based on your specific situation. Both options deliver the same design quality and conversion performance.
What do you need from me to get started?
We need three things: your brand guidelines (logo, fonts, colors, tone of voice), access to your current analytics so we can review existing page performance, and a completed brief or a 30-minute strategy call to define your goals, audience, and offer. If you do not have brand guidelines, we will establish the visual direction during the design phase. If you have existing ad creative, share that too so we can ensure message match between your ads and the landing page. The more context you provide upfront, the faster we move through the design phase.
How many rounds of revisions are included?
Every project includes two rounds of revisions at the wireframe stage and two rounds at the visual design stage. This structure ensures we lock in the right page structure before investing time in pixel-level design, and that the final design is exactly what you want before development begins. Development revisions cover any discrepancies between the approved design and the built page. If you need additional revision rounds beyond what is included, we accommodate them at a transparent hourly rate discussed upfront.
Do you offer A/B testing services?
Yes. We design and build A/B test variants as a standalone service or as part of an ongoing optimization engagement. Each test variant is designed around a single, clearly defined hypothesis so you get clean data on what drives the improvement. We can integrate with your existing testing platform (Google Optimize, VWO, Optimizely, Convert, or native Webflow A/B testing) or recommend the right tool for your traffic volume and budget. We also provide a test analysis report after each experiment with recommendations for the next iteration.
Are your landing pages optimized for mobile?
Every page we design and build is mobile-first. We design the mobile layout as a first-class experience, not a scaled-down version of the desktop page. This means touch-friendly tap targets, optimized image sizes for cellular connections, thumb-zone-aware CTA placement, and content prioritization that accounts for the shorter attention spans on mobile. With 60 to 70 percent of landing page traffic now coming from mobile devices, mobile performance directly impacts your conversion rate and cost per acquisition.
Will my landing page be SEO-friendly?
Yes. Every page is built with clean semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, meta tags, Open Graph tags, structured data where applicable, optimized image alt text, and fast load times. These are the technical foundations that search engines reward. However, most landing pages are designed for paid traffic campaigns where SEO is secondary to conversion rate. If organic search is a primary traffic source for your page, we will adjust the content strategy and page structure accordingly during the research phase to balance SEO requirements with conversion optimization.
What does a landing page project typically cost?
Landing page projects start at $3,000 for a single-page design and Webflow build. A typical project with research, custom design, Webflow or custom development, copywriting framework, analytics setup, and post-launch support falls in the $4,000 to $8,000 range. Complex pages with custom animations, multi-step forms, or extensive integrations can go higher. We are based in Portugal, which means you get senior-level design and development talent at rates that are 40 to 60 percent lower than comparable US or UK agencies. Every project receives a fixed-price quote after the strategy call so there are no surprises.
