What We Produce

Every brand needs a different mix of creative formats. We produce the full spectrum of video ad types so you can test broadly, find your winners, and scale what works. Here is exactly what we deliver.

UGC-Style Ads

Authentic, creator-driven content that looks native to the feed. Real people talking about your product in their own words, filmed on their own phones. This format consistently outperforms polished brand content on Meta and TikTok because it feels like a recommendation from a friend, not an advertisement. We brief creators with proven hook structures and talking points while leaving room for genuine delivery that algorithms reward with lower CPMs.

Product Demos

Clear, compelling demonstrations that show your product in action. Whether it is a physical item being unboxed and used or a software walkthrough with screen recording, product demos remove the guesswork for potential buyers. We shoot these with close-up detail shots, clean lighting, and tight editing so viewers understand the value proposition in under 30 seconds. These work especially well for mid-funnel retargeting on Meta and YouTube.

Testimonial Videos

Nothing sells like social proof. We coordinate with your existing customers or match you with creators who genuinely use products in your category. Each testimonial follows a proven structure: the problem they had, how they found your product, and the specific results they experienced. We capture these in both short-form (15-30 seconds) for TikTok and longer-form (60-90 seconds) for YouTube Shorts and Meta.

Before/After Transformations

The most psychologically compelling format in performance marketing. Show the problem state, introduce your product, and reveal the result. This format drives some of the highest click-through rates we see across all categories because it creates an immediate emotional response. Particularly effective for skincare, fitness, home improvement, cleaning products, and any category where visual transformation is possible.

Unboxing & First Impressions

Capture the excitement of receiving and opening your product for the first time. Unboxing videos tap into the anticipation and discovery that drives impulse purchases. We film these with multiple camera angles and genuine reactions, then cut them into fast-paced, attention-grabbing sequences. The first three seconds always feature a compelling hook because that is where 70% of viewers decide to keep watching or scroll past.

Lifestyle & Talking Head

Lifestyle content places your product in real-world context, showing how it fits into daily routines and solves everyday problems. Talking head videos pair a creator directly addressing the camera with B-roll footage that reinforces the message. We combine both formats with text overlays, captions, and trending audio to maximize engagement. These are the workhorse formats for top-of-funnel awareness campaigns on every major platform.

AI-Enhanced Creatives

We use AI tools to extend what is possible in post-production: background replacement, visual effects, dynamic text animations, and intelligent scene transitions. This is not about replacing human creators. It is about making each piece of footage work harder. A single shoot can produce three to five distinct visual treatments, each testing a different aesthetic or messaging angle. This multiplies your creative volume without multiplying your production budget.

Platform-Native Formats

Every platform has different specs, different audience behaviors, and different algorithmic preferences. We do not just resize your ads. We re-edit them for each platform. TikTok gets faster cuts, trending sounds, and native text styling. Meta gets optimized hooks and aspect ratios for Stories, Reels, and Feed. YouTube Shorts gets slightly longer builds with stronger retention hooks. Each version is a standalone creative optimized for where it will run.

Our Process

We have refined this workflow across hundreds of campaigns. Every step is designed to move fast without cutting corners. Most clients go from kickoff to first deliverables in seven business days.

Step 01

Brief & Strategy

We start by understanding your product, your audience, and your current ad performance. You fill out our creative brief or hop on a 30-minute strategy call where we audit your existing ads, identify gaps in your creative mix, and define the formats, angles, and hooks most likely to move your key metrics. We research your competitors and analyze what is working in your category right now. By the end of this step, we have a clear production plan with specific concepts, scripts, and a creator shortlist tailored to your brand.

Step 02

Script Development

Our copywriters develop scripts using proven direct-response frameworks. Every script starts with a hook designed to stop the scroll in the first 1.5 seconds, followed by a problem-agitation sequence, your product as the solution, social proof or a specific benefit, and a clear call to action. We typically write three to five script variations per concept so you have multiple angles to test. You review and approve before anything goes into production. We also prepare visual shot lists and text overlay copy for the editing team.

Step 03

Creator Matching

We maintain a vetted network of over 600 UGC creators across demographics, niches, and content styles. Based on your brand voice and target audience, we select creators who authentically match your product category. You get profile cards with sample work, audience demographics, and past performance data for each recommended creator. Once you approve the selection, we handle all creator coordination: contracts, product shipping, briefing, and timeline management. No chasing freelancers or managing spreadsheets on your end.

Step 04

Production

Creators film according to the approved scripts and shot lists. We provide detailed filming guidelines covering lighting, framing, audio quality, and platform-specific requirements. Every creator submits raw footage for quality review before we move to editing. If anything does not meet our standards, we request reshoots immediately. For product demos and studio content, we handle production in-house with professional equipment. This step typically takes three to four days, and we can run multiple creators simultaneously to compress the timeline further.

Step 05

Editing & Optimization

Our editors cut each video with performance in mind. That means tight pacing, dynamic text overlays, branded lower thirds, sound design, and platform-native formatting. We create multiple versions of each core creative: different hooks, different lengths (15, 30, and 60 seconds), and different aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, and 4:5). Each version is a distinct testable asset, not just a crop. We also generate thumbnail stills from key frames for use in static ad placements. You receive organized deliverables labeled by concept, creator, platform, and duration.

Step 06

Testing & Iteration

Delivery is not the finish line. We provide a testing framework that tells you exactly how to structure your ad sets for maximum learning. After your first round of spend, we analyze performance data with you: which hooks are stopping the scroll, which creators drive the most conversions, which formats deliver the best ROAS. We use these insights to inform the next production batch, doubling down on what works and replacing what does not. Clients who commit to iterative testing cycles consistently see ROAS improvements of two to three times within the first 60 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive the finished videos?

Do I own the content you produce?
Yes. You receive full ownership and usage rights for all deliverables, including raw footage, edited videos, scripts, and thumbnail stills. There are no licensing fees, no royalties, and no usage restrictions. You can run the content on any platform, repurpose it for organic social, embed it on your website, or use it in email marketing. Creator agreements include full commercial usage rights as part of the production fee.
Can I choose specific creators for my brand?
Absolutely. During the creator matching phase, we present you with a shortlist of recommended creators complete with profile cards, sample work, and demographic information. What platforms do you optimize videos for?
We deliver platform-specific versions for Meta (Facebook and Instagram Feed, Stories, and Reels), TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat, Pinterest, and LinkedIn. Each platform version is re-edited to match that platform's best practices, not just resized. TikTok versions get faster pacing and native text styling. Meta versions get optimized hooks for autoplay environments. YouTube Shorts versions get slightly longer retention builds. We include all platform versions in every tier at no extra charge.
What if I am not happy with the videos?
Every tier includes revision rounds. Starter includes one round, Growth includes two rounds, and Scale includes unlimited revisions. A revision round covers changes to editing, text overlays, music, pacing, and color grading. If a fundamental creative direction change is needed, we will reshoot with the same creator at no cost. Our approval rate on first delivery is above 90% because we align closely during the brief and script approval stages before any filming begins.

Our core service is creative production, not media buying. However, we provide a detailed testing playbook with every order that shows you exactly how to structure your ad sets, which metrics to monitor, and when to scale or kill each creative. Hiring freelancers means you manage the entire workflow yourself: finding creators, negotiating rates, writing briefs, coordinating shipping, reviewing footage, managing revisions, and handling contracts.
