Brand Systems

What's Included

Every brand identity project is a system, not a single asset. Here is what you receive when you work with us, with every element designed to work together and scale across every touchpoint your brand will ever need.

Logo Design

Your logo is the anchor of your entire visual identity. We design a primary logo, a secondary (stacked or horizontal) variation, a standalone icon or brandmark, and a monochrome version for single-color applications. Each version is tested across backgrounds, sizes, and contexts to ensure it performs everywhere, from a 16-pixel favicon to a billboard. You receive vector files (SVG, AI, EPS) and raster exports (PNG, JPG) in every configuration.

Color System

Color drives recognition faster than any other visual element. We define your primary brand color, a secondary palette of 2-3 supporting hues, and accent colors for CTAs, highlights, and interactive states. Every color comes with exact HEX, RGB, CMYK, and Pantone values, plus clear usage rules: background ratios, minimum contrast requirements for accessibility (WCAG AA), and combinations to avoid. No guesswork for your team or any vendor who touches the brand.

Typography System

We select and pair a heading typeface and a body typeface that reinforce your brand personality, whether that is authoritative and sharp, warm and approachable, or bold and contemporary. The system includes a full sizing scale (H1 through body text and captions), line-height and letter-spacing specifications, weight usage rules, and web-safe fallback stacks. If you need a custom or licensed font, we handle the sourcing and provide licensing guidance.

Brand Guidelines Document

The guidelines document is the single source of truth for your brand. At 40 to 60 pages, it covers logo usage and clear-space rules, color specifications, typography hierarchy, photography and imagery direction, tone of voice principles, and real-world application examples. This document ensures every designer, developer, marketer, and external vendor who works with your brand produces consistent, on-brand materials without needing to ask you how things should look.

Visual Elements

Brands are more than logos and colors. We develop a library of supporting visual elements that give your identity depth and texture: custom patterns or geometric motifs, textural overlays, a cohesive iconography style (outline, filled, or duotone), and direction for illustration or photography treatment. These elements transform a flat identity into a rich, dimensional brand world your audience can feel across every surface.

Brand Applications

We bring your identity to life on the materials you actually use. Standard applications include business card design (front and back), letterhead and envelope layout, email signature template, and social media profile and cover image templates for LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and X. Each application file is delivered print-ready or pixel-perfect for digital, so you can go live the day we deliver, without spending another dollar on production design.

Our Process

We follow a five-phase process that moves from research through strategy into design. Every phase has a defined deliverable and a feedback checkpoint, so you always know exactly where we are and what comes next. No disappearing for weeks. No surprises at the reveal.

Phase 01

Discovery & Research

We start with a deep-dive into your business, your market, and your audience. This phase includes a brand audit of your current materials (if they exist), a competitive landscape analysis covering 5 to 8 direct competitors, audience persona development, and a stakeholder interview to surface the ambitions and constraints that shape the brand. The output is a research brief that grounds every creative decision we make from this point forward. Duration: 3 to 5 business days.

Phase 02

Strategy & Positioning

Before we open a design tool, we define the strategic foundation. We establish your brand archetype (the psychological framework that drives emotional resonance), articulate your value proposition in a single sentence, map your positioning against competitors, and set the guardrails for tone of voice and messaging style. This is the phase where the brand goes from abstract to intentional. Duration: 2 to 3 business days.

Phase 03

Concept Development

We develop three distinct creative directions, each presented as a mood board with preliminary logo concepts, color palettes, type pairings, and example applications. These are not minor variations of the same idea. They are genuinely different interpretations of your brand strategy, giving you a meaningful choice. You select one direction, and you can pull elements from the others if something resonates. Duration: 4 to 5 business days.

Phase 04

Design & Refinement

The chosen direction gets developed into the full identity system: finalized logo in all variations, complete color palette, typography scale, visual elements, and initial brand applications. You receive two rounds of refinement to adjust details, tweak proportions, and polish. We present everything in context, on realistic mockups, so you see how the brand behaves in the real world, not just as isolated assets on a white screen. Duration: 5 to 7 business days.

Phase 05

Delivery & Brand Guidelines

We compile the complete brand guidelines document, export all final assets in every file format you need (vector, raster, print-ready, web-optimized), and organize everything in a clearly labeled folder structure. The handoff includes a 30-minute walkthrough call where we walk your team through the guidelines, explain the rationale behind each decision, and answer questions about implementation. You leave this phase with everything you need to deploy the brand across every channel, immediately. Duration: 2 to 3 business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from clients considering a brand identity project.

How long does brand identity design take?

Our standard timeline is three to four weeks for the Professional tier, from kickoff to final file delivery. The Essential package can be completed in two weeks, and Enterprise projects typically run four to six weeks depending on the number of sub-brands and the depth of the strategy phase. These timelines assume timely feedback at each checkpoint. We schedule review windows in advance so there are no bottlenecks on your side.

What if I already have a logo?

If your existing logo is strong but the rest of your brand identity is inconsistent or undeveloped, we can build the full system around your current mark. This includes color system, typography, visual elements, brand applications, and guidelines. We will assess the logo during the Discovery phase and let you know honestly whether it can serve as the foundation for a cohesive system or whether a refresh would significantly improve the outcome. There is no pressure either way. The decision is yours.

How many concepts will I see?

You will see three distinct creative directions during the Concept Development phase. Each direction includes a mood board, preliminary logo concepts, color palette, type pairing, and example applications. These are not minor variations of one idea. They are genuinely different interpretations of your brand strategy. You choose one direction to develop, and you are welcome to pull specific elements from the other directions if something resonates.

What file formats do I receive?

You receive everything you need for both digital and print. Logo files are delivered in SVG, AI (Adobe Illustrator), EPS, PNG (transparent and on-background), and JPG. Color specifications include HEX, RGB, CMYK, and Pantone references. Brand guidelines are delivered as a designed PDF. Application templates are provided in their native format (Figma, Illustrator, or InDesign) plus export-ready PDF. Social media templates are delivered as Figma files with export presets configured for each platform.

Do you do brand naming?

We do not offer brand naming as a standalone service, but we can recommend naming partners we have worked with. If you are actively deciding between two or three name options, we are happy to weigh in from a design perspective during the Discovery phase, since some names lend themselves to stronger visual identities than others. The identity design process begins once the name is finalized.

Can you create brand guidelines for an existing identity?

Yes. If you already have a logo, colors, and fonts but lack a formal guidelines document, we can audit your existing assets, identify inconsistencies, fill gaps in the system, and produce a comprehensive brand guidelines document that standardizes everything. This is particularly common for companies that have grown organically and accumulated brand materials from different designers over the years. Pricing for guidelines-only projects starts at $3,000 depending on the complexity of the existing identity.

What's the difference between a logo and a brand identity?

A logo is a single mark. A brand identity is the complete visual system that makes a company recognizable across every touchpoint. It includes the logo, but also the color palette, typography, visual elements, photography direction, tone of voice, and the rules governing how all of these work together. A logo alone cannot create consistency. A brand identity can. Think of the logo as a signature and the brand identity as the entire personality behind it. Companies that invest only in a logo almost always end up with inconsistent, fragmented visuals within six months.
