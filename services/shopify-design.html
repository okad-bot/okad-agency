What We Build

Every Shopify store has different needs depending on the product, the audience, and the brand. We design and build every layer of the storefront to work together as a cohesive, high-converting shopping experience.

Custom Theme Design

We design bespoke Shopify themes from scratch that reflect your brand identity and stand apart from the thousands of stores running the same templates. Every layout decision is informed by conversion data and user behavior patterns. You get a store that looks like a premium brand experience, not a cookie-cutter template with your logo swapped in. We deliver clean, maintainable theme code built on Shopify 2.0 architecture so your team can make content updates without touching code.

Product Page Optimization

Your product pages are where buying decisions happen. We design them to answer every question a hesitant buyer might have: clear product photography layouts, benefit-driven descriptions, trust badges, size guides, and social proof sections positioned exactly where they reduce friction. We structure product pages with a visual hierarchy that guides the eye from headline to add-to-cart button. A/B tested layouts ensure each element earns its place on the page.

Collection & Category Pages

Well-designed collection pages make browsing effortless and help shoppers find what they want faster. We build filterable, sortable collection layouts with clear product cards, quick-view functionality, and smart merchandising options. Each collection page is designed to reduce decision fatigue and encourage exploration. We also implement dynamic collections that automatically update based on tags, inventory, and seasonal rules so your store stays fresh without manual curation.

Checkout & Cart UX

Cart abandonment kills revenue. We redesign the entire cart-to-checkout flow to eliminate every unnecessary step and moment of hesitation. That means clear order summaries, trust signals at every stage, progress indicators, and mobile-optimized form fields that autofill correctly. We implement upsell and cross-sell modules in the cart drawer that increase average order value without feeling pushy. For Shopify Plus merchants, we customize the checkout page itself with branded elements and conversion-focused layouts.

Mobile-First Responsive Design

Over 70% of Shopify traffic comes from mobile devices. We design mobile-first, meaning every layout, interaction, and content block is optimized for thumb-friendly navigation on small screens before we scale up to desktop. Touch targets are appropriately sized, images load fast with lazy loading and responsive srcsets, and the mobile checkout flow is streamlined to minimize typing. Your store will look and perform beautifully on every device from a 4-inch phone screen to a 27-inch monitor.

App Integration & Setup

The right Shopify apps can transform your store's functionality, but poor integration creates bloat, conflicts, and slow load times. We audit, select, and integrate the apps your store actually needs: reviews, loyalty programs, subscriptions, email capture, analytics, and shipping solutions. Every app is configured to match your theme design seamlessly so nothing looks bolted on. We also optimize app loading order to protect your page speed scores, because a one-second delay in load time can reduce conversions by up to 7%.

Our Process

We follow a structured process refined across hundreds of Shopify builds. Every phase has clear deliverables and approval gates so you know exactly where your project stands at all times.

Step 01

Discovery & Audit

We start by understanding your brand, your products, your customers, and your business goals. If you have an existing store, we run a comprehensive audit covering design, UX, page speed, SEO, and conversion funnel performance. We analyze your competitors and identify what the top-performing stores in your niche are doing right. By the end of discovery, we deliver a detailed findings report with prioritized recommendations and a project roadmap that maps design decisions directly to revenue opportunities.

Step 02

Wireframing & UX

Before any visual design begins, we map out the user experience with wireframes for every key page: homepage, collection pages, product pages, cart, and checkout. Wireframes let us test information architecture, navigation flow, and content hierarchy without getting distracted by colors and typography. We use real product data and realistic content blocks so you can evaluate layouts in context, not with placeholder lorem ipsum text. You review and approve the wireframes before we move into visual design.

Step 03

Visual Design

This is where your brand comes to life on screen. We create high-fidelity mockups in Figma for desktop and mobile that translate your brand identity into a complete Shopify storefront. Every detail is considered: typography scales, color application, spacing rhythms, button styles, hover states, and micro-interactions. You see exactly how your store will look and feel before a single line of code is written. We present two to three design directions and refine your chosen direction through focused revision rounds until it is pixel-perfect.

Step 04

Shopify Development

Our developers build your approved designs into a fully functional Shopify theme using clean, semantic Liquid code and modern front-end practices. We develop on a staging environment so you can review progress without affecting your live store. Every section is built as a customizable Shopify 2.0 block so you can rearrange, show, hide, and edit content from the theme editor without developer help. We integrate all required apps, set up collections, configure shipping and tax rules, and populate your store with real products.

Step 05

Launch & Optimization

Before going live, we run a comprehensive launch checklist: cross-browser testing, mobile device testing, page speed optimization, SEO configuration, analytics setup, payment gateway verification, and order flow testing. We handle the DNS transition and theme publish to make launch day smooth and stress-free. After launch, we monitor performance for two weeks, fix any issues that surface with real traffic, and deliver a post-launch optimization report with data-driven recommendations for ongoing improvements to your conversion rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with Shopify Plus?
Yes. We work with both standard Shopify and Shopify Plus. For Plus merchants, we take advantage of the additional customization options including checkout.liquid customization, Shopify Scripts for dynamic pricing and discounts, multi-currency and multi-language storefronts, and advanced API integrations. If you are considering upgrading to Plus, we can advise on whether the additional features justify the cost for your specific business needs and help manage the migration.
How long does a typical Shopify store project take?
Most projects take two to four weeks from kickoff to launch. A straightforward theme customization with standard pages can be completed in two weeks. A fully custom design with multiple collection templates, custom product page layouts, and app integrations typically takes three to four weeks. Complex builds with Shopify Plus checkout customization, custom app development, or migration from another platform may take four to six weeks. We provide a detailed timeline during the discovery phase so you know exactly what to expect.
What does a Shopify store design project typically cost?
Our Shopify projects typically range from $4,000 to $15,000 depending on scope and complexity. A theme customization with optimized product pages and standard sections starts around $4,000. A fully custom design with bespoke layouts, multiple templates, and app integrations ranges from $7,000 to $12,000. Enterprise-level Shopify Plus builds with checkout customization and advanced functionality start at $12,000. We provide a detailed fixed-price quote after the discovery call so there are no surprises.
Can you migrate my store from another platform to Shopify?
Yes. We handle migrations from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Squarespace, Wix, and custom-built platforms. We migrate your products, customers, order history, blog content, and URL structures. SEO is a critical concern during any migration, so we set up 301 redirects for every existing URL to preserve your search rankings and incoming links. We also handle domain transfer and DNS configuration. The migration runs in parallel with the design work so the timeline is not significantly longer than a new build.
Do you build custom Shopify apps?
Yes. If your store needs functionality that existing apps do not provide or if you want to reduce app dependencies for performance reasons, we can build custom Shopify apps. Common custom builds include product configurators, subscription logic, custom pricing rules, loyalty programs, and integrations with third-party systems like ERP or warehouse management software. Custom apps are scoped and quoted separately from the design project. We recommend exhausting existing app options first since custom development adds cost and maintenance overhead.
How do you handle SEO for Shopify stores?
SEO is built into every store we design, not bolted on as an afterthought. We configure proper heading hierarchies, structured data markup, clean URL structures, optimized meta titles and descriptions, alt text for all images, and fast page load times. We also set up a proper sitemap, configure robots.txt, and implement canonical tags to prevent duplicate content issues. For migrations, we create comprehensive redirect maps. Our stores consistently score above 90 on Google PageSpeed Insights because search engines reward fast, well-structured sites with better rankings.
Do you offer post-launch support and maintenance?
Yes. Every project includes two weeks of post-launch support at no additional cost to address any issues that arise with real traffic. Beyond that, we offer monthly retainer packages for ongoing design updates, seasonal refreshes, new page templates, app management, and performance optimization. Retainer clients get priority response times and a team that already understands their codebase and brand. We also offer one-off support for specific tasks like adding new product templates, building landing pages, or integrating new tools as your business grows.
